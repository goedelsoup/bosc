An in-browser LLM over the corpus — does it earn its weight?

This prototype puts the two halves of yidam export web side by side against 238 real Lima mirror nodes: an in-browser retrieval index (cheap, grounded, cites its sources) and a WebLLM chat model that generates prose from what retrieval finds (heavy, WebGPU-only, and prone to inventing citations against evidence that is litigation record).

Corpus: corpus.json — projected by watermark corpus-mirror (Epic #1560 E1/#1561), concept 77 · relation 60 · artifact 71 · question 27 · hypothesis 3. The retrieval pane needs no network. The chat pane downloads a model from a CDN and needs WebGPU.

Generate with WebLLM

Under evaluation
What this costs. The model runs entirely in your browser via WebGPU. First load downloads the whole model (0.4–0.9 GB below), then caches it. It needs a discrete/modern GPU and a WebGPU-capable browser. A 0.5–1B model is far weaker than the Claude behind /api/ask and will paraphrase or invent — treat its output as a demo of the mechanics, not as a reading of the record.

The system prompt restricts the model to the retrieved nodes and asks it to cite node ids and to say when the record is silent — the same discipline /api/ask enforces. Watch how well a small in-browser model actually honours it.
