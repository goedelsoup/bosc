## Known gaps & caveats

- **`grid-profile.yaml`'s `load_share` is a screening magnitude, not a disclosure.** It serializes
  the **central** of the `[inference]` floor-area IT-load screen (113.75 MW → 143.9 MW facility draw)
  as a share of the utility/BA/state denominators (8.21% of DP&L retail, 0.139% of PJM, 0.7% of Ohio).
  It carries the central point only — not the 52.5 / 175 MW low/high bounds, which live in
  `it_load_citation` and `demand-pressure.yaml` — since the campus's actual load is undisclosed (see
  the IT-load caveat below).
- **EIA requires a key** (`WATERMARK_EIA_API_KEY`, free). Without one, `watermark eia` replays the
  committed offline fixtures (`tests/fixtures/economics/eia/`); a live keyed pull refreshes to the
  latest annual vintage. Figures here are EIA **annual averages** (latest stable annual; EIA annual
  data lags ~1 year) — the committed 2025-vintage pull (2026-07-11/2026-07-12).
- **The campus buys at wholesale/industrial rates, not the residential price** shown in
  `demand-pressure.yaml` — that price is the *consumer-impact reference*, not the campus's own bill.
- **`demand-pressure.yaml`'s IT load is an `[inference]` floor-area screening bracket** (52.5 / 113.75
  / 175 MW), not a disclosed permit load — see `data/extracted/troy-piqua/data-centers.md` and
  `it_load_citation` on `SiteProfile.facility`. The price-pressure band is a **stylized screening
  sensitivity, not a forecast**.
