# Troy–Piqua GIS — committed reference data

City of Piqua / Miami County (OH) GIS layers for the Troy–Piqua watershed point
(`watermark.sites` `troy-piqua`). Schema-driven via the per-site `gis_*` field-maps (#237); see
[`docs/onboarding.md`](../../../docs/onboarding.md) and
[`src/watermark/connectors/gis_schema.py`](../../../src/watermark/connectors/gis_schema.py). This
is the shared `reference_dir` for the site's `gis_zoning`, `gis_parcel`, and `gis_flood` schemas.

## `zoning-districts.yaml` — zoning-district catalog (#830)

- **Source:** City of Piqua GIS — ArcGIS Online hosted FeatureServer `Zoning_Districts_public_view`
  (org `kZPPWTIJ6kOFJTWc`), layer 17 `Code Piqua`. Pulled 2026-07-13; values verbatim from the service.
  `source_url`: <https://services8.arcgis.com/kZPPWTIJ6kOFJTWc/arcgis/rest/services/Zoning_Districts_public_view/FeatureServer/17>
- **What it is:** the 26 districts of the City of Piqua's form-based code, by `CPZoneDist` label,
  with a polygon count each (9,758 polygons total). `IH` (Industrial Heavy) and `IL` (Industrial
  Light) are the two industrial districts.
- **Situs check (the #830 acceptance criterion):** the layer carries the dashed auditor `PARCEL`
  id, so per-parcel joins work. Project Klondike / J5 campus parcel **`N44-101770` returns `IH`**
  and the adjacent **`N44-101808` returns `IL`** — confirming the campus footprint sits in the
  City of Piqua's heavy/light-industrial districts, matching the "annexed and zoned
  heavy-industrial" record in [`data/extracted/troy-piqua/data-centers.md`](../../extracted/troy-piqua/data-centers.md).

### Known gaps & caveats

- **City limits only.** Coverage is the City of Piqua; unincorporated Miami County townships (and
  the City of Troy) carry their own separate zoning layers, not this one. An `in_city=false` result
  is a verified outside-city answer, not a missing lookup.
- **Currency (2025-02 snapshot).** The layer's rows were last edited 2025-02, so the two J5 campus
  parcels re-platted/conveyed in Dec 2025 (`N44-101834`, `N44-101846`) are **not yet in this
  snapshot** — only `N44-101770` is (as `IH`). Re-pull when the City refreshes the layer.
- **`polygon_count` is polygons, not parcels** — a single parcel may carry more than one polygon.

## Parcels — the Miami County `parcel_joined` layer (#1483)

The parcel layer (`gis_parcel = MIAMI_PARCEL_SCHEMA`) is the Miami County ArcGIS Online
`parcel_joined` FeatureServer (org `wCWf4EGMg4PzHwzA`, auditor CAMA + geometry). The reviewed
campus assemblage is committed separately as
[`data/reference/troy-piqua/parcel-assemblage.geojson`](../troy-piqua/parcel-assemblage.geojson)
(the three J5 LLC parcels, 607.8 ac); no bulk county parcel dump is committed here.
`source_url`: <https://services3.arcgis.com/wCWf4EGMg4PzHwzA/arcgis/rest/services/parcel_joined/FeatureServer/0>

## Floodzone — the shared national FEMA NFHL

`gis_flood` is the shared `NATIONAL_NFHL_FLOOD_SCHEMA` (this `reference_dir`). No committed
catalog: a national `1=1` catalog is not meaningful — flood is a spatial query against the
identified footprint (`floodzone --footprint`), which reads the committed campus geometry.
