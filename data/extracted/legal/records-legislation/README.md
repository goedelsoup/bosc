# Public-records legislation — reviewed reads

Reviewed reads of legislation that would amend **R.C. 149.43**, the statute this
corpus's public-records work rests on. Source bills live under
[`data/documents/legal/records-legislation/`](../../../documents/legal/records-legislation/README.md).

## Contents

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `hb314-149-43.digest.yaml` | Provision-by-provision structured read of **Sub. H.B. 314 (136th G.A., As Passed by the House)** — the three new mechanisms (harass/disrupt action; voluminous-request contractor; the hours-tier fee schedule), what it leaves unchanged, the relevance-to-the-relator synthesis, and a `substitute_changes` block diffing the As-Introduced vs As-Passed versions (including the stale (B)(11) cross-reference drafting error). |

Two source versions are ingested under
[`documents/legal/records-legislation/`](../../../documents/legal/records-legislation/README.md):
`hb314_00_IN.pdf` (As Introduced) and `hb314_02_PH.pdf` (Sub., As Passed). The

analysis memo.

## Conventions

Provision reads are `[verified]` against the cited division/page of the bill text;
the relevance synthesis is `[inference]`; unresolved items (exemption fit, enacted
text) are `[open]`. The operative reads track the **As-Passed-by-the-House** version,
which is not enacted law — verify against current R.C. 149.43 before relying on any
read in a filing.
