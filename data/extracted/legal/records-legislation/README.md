corpus's public-records work rests on. Source bills live under
[`data/documents/legal/records-legislation/`](../../../documents/legal/records-legislation/README.md).

| Path | What |

## Conventions

Provision reads are `[verified]` against the cited division/page of the bill text;
the relevance synthesis is `[inference]`; unresolved items (exemption fit, enacted
text) are `[open]`. This is the **As-Passed-by-the-House** version, not enacted law —
verify against current R.C. 149.43 before relying on any read in a filing.
