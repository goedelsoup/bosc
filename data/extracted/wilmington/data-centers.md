# Wilmington / Clinton Co., OH — Data-Center Activity Register

Discover-and-pin register for the Wilmington watershed point (Lytle Creek → Todd Fork →
**Little Miami**). Status **as of 2026-07-11**. Tags are BOSC evidentiary discipline:
`[verified]` = cited primary/government source, `[reported]` = credible secondary,
`[reference]`, `[inference]`, `[open]`.

**This register supersedes the 2026-07-03 scaffold and inverts its premise.** The prior
flat-no-activity finding reflected only an empty corpus (#519 first pass): publicly disclosed
since Nov 2025 are a ~471-acre / **$4B Amazon Data Services Inc.** campus on the former
**Cosler Farm** (§1) and a second ~545-acre **Ardent/TAC** rezoning corridor (§2) — together
~1,000+ acres of one US-68 S / SR-730 corridor `[inference — the §1 ~471-ac and §2 545.893-ac
figures summed]`. Neither is on Air Park land; the
**Wilmington Air Park (ILN)** remains the *cargo* comparator #519 framed (§3). The web
research sweep ran **2026-07-11** (#1277 grooming refresh); committing this register and
pulling its instruments is tracked as **#1471**, which closes #519. **Nothing here is
fabricated**; every figure is cited or marked `[open]`.

## 1 — Amazon Data Services Inc. / "Cosler Farm" campus (1488 S US-68)

- `[verified]` **AWS project disclosed Nov 2025**: $4B investment on a **471-acre** parcel

  Services Inc.** (the name under which it intervened in the Sharp litigation, §1.2). SOS

  AWS — not guided by the Port Authority, the City, or any public entity (FAQ). Consistent

  **2025-08-07** (FAQ).
- `[verified]` AWS selected the site **for proximity to the City's wastewater treatment
  plant** (FAQ) — the water/sewer thread is constitutive, not incidental.
- `[reported]` Site plan revised **9 → 12 buildings**; the two versions are unreconciled —

  (§1.5).
- `[reported]` **Direct evaporative cooling**, projected **~6M gal/yr** cooling-water
  consumption.

  **US-68 and SR-730**.
- `[reported]` AWS buys water from the City at **residential rates plus a reserve fee**
  (WYSO); the reserved capacity (MGD) is **undisclosed** `[open]` — the real abstraction

### 1.2 — The Sharp litigation (the live thread)

- `[verified]` ***Sharp et al. v. City of Wilmington, Ohio et al.*, S.D. Ohio
  1:26-cv-00448** (govinfo docket). `[reported]` Filed 2026-03-25 in Clinton County Common
  Pleas by plaintiffs ~200 ft from the Cosler Farm site; amended 2026-04-21 (due-process
  claims); removed to federal court, Judge Jeffery Hopkins. PACER pull `[open]` (#1471).
- `[reported — wnewsj 2026-05-29]` **Amazon Data Services Inc.** moved to intervene
  2026-05-22; granted defendant-intervenor Jun 2026.
- `[reported — wnewsj 2026-07-09]` A **three-day evidentiary hearing** concluded 2026-07-09.
- `[reported — WCPO/WDTN, converging, ~2026-07-09/10]` The court ordered the City to **redo
  three ordinances** — the data-center zoning-text amendment, the generator-noise exemption,
  and the Cosler Farm map rezoning — for failure to give the required **30-day mailed
  notice**; re-run hearings must allow expert testimony, cross-examination, and counsel.
  **The ruling itself is unpulled** `[open]` — cite exact holdings and ordinance numbers only
  from the order (PACER/govinfo, #1471).

### 1.3 — Water / hydrology hook

- **Max withdrawal:** undisclosed — AWS water reservation (MGD) is the operative figure.
  `[open]` (#1472)
- **Projected cooling-water consumption:** ~6M gal/yr, direct evaporative cooling.
  `[reported]` The ~16,400 GPD average is arithmetic from that annual figure (6M gal ÷ 365 d).
  `[inference]` Peak-day draw is a distinct, undisclosed number. `[open]`
- **Water source:** City of Wilmington municipal system. The City's principal raw supply
  **since 1994 is Caesar Creek Lake** (USACE-built), with the two city-owned **Burtonville
  reservoirs** (450M gal total, pumped from Cowan Creek) as backup — **not Todd Fork**.
  `[verified — City water-treatment page]` The USACE/ODNR storage-contract terms are the
  instrument still to pull, and an AG dispute over the Corps' charges is on the record
  `[reported — wnewsj]`; #1472 tracks the pull (this explains profile gage `03242350`).
- **Wastewater:** to the City WWTP — **OH0028134 / 1PD00013\*QD**, outfall 001 to Lytle
  Creek at RM 6.83 (Lytle Creek → Todd Fork → Little Miami); design flow 3.0 MGD, **PTI
  #1543170 expansion to 4.5 MGD**, new limits effective 2026-03-01. `[verified — Ohio EPA
  NPDES fact sheet 1PD00013.fs, 2023-05-19]`
- **Site process/stormwater discharge:** likely path is the statewide data-center general
  permit **OHD000001**, still draft (§1.5). `[inference]` Coverage on the record `[open]`.

### 1.4 — Hydrology screen

- **Receiving water (effluent, indirect):** Lytle Creek → **Todd Fork** → Little Miami. Todd
  Fork is **ungaged**; the at-site 7Q10 is a documented drainage-area-ratio method **awaiting
  its drainage-area inputs** — see `data/extracted/wilmington/low-flow-screen.md` (#516). No
  7Q10 figure exists yet to screen against. `[open]`
- **Withdrawal vs. effluent are distinct screens** `[inference]`: the withdrawal side
  stresses **Caesar Creek Lake storage** (USACE contract terms), the effluent side the
  Lytle Creek / Todd Fork reach. Do not mix them (#1472).
- **Abstraction vs. 7Q10:** not computable — numerator (reserved MGD) and denominator
  (at-site 7Q10) are both open. Fill from the water contract + StreamStats pulls only. `[open]`
- **Overlay:** the Little Miami is a **National & State Scenic River** (state-designated
  1969/1971, Ohio's first; national 1980) — an anti-degradation overlay on any new/expanded
  discharge. `[verified — ODNR / rivers.gov]`
