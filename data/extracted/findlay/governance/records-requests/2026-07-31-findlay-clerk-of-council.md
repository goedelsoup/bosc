## Why this request exists

Acceptance criterion 1 of issue 1463 asks for Ordinance 2026-42's certified text and the
council journal for 2026-03-17, 2026-04-07 and 2026-04-22, with the banned-use definition
recorded verbatim. None of it could be obtained from the open web: `findlayohio.gov` and
`findlayohio.com` return HTTP 403 to automated requests on every path tried, American Legal's
Findlay code library returns 403, and there is no provisioned Legistar tenant. That is an
access failure, not a refusal — nobody has denied anything, because nobody has been asked. So
the next step is to ask.

The pre-annexation-agreement item is criterion 3, which asks that this request be filed and its
response logged.

## Scoping notes (why the request is written the way it is)

The request is narrow on purpose. A records request that sweeps ("all documents relating to
data centers") invites a delay or an overbreadth denial and gives the agency a reason not to
start. Each item below names an instrument type, a body and a date range, so each can be
answered independently and a partial response is still useful. Item 5 is the one broad item and
it is bounded by subject and by a 19-month window.

Nothing here asks for anything exempt on its face: no personnel files, no attorney-client
material, no security records. If the city withholds under an exemption, R.C. 149.43(B)(1)
requires it to identify the exemption, and the log below records that.

## Draft text

> **Public Records Request — R.C. 149.43**
>
> To the Clerk of Council, City of Findlay:
>
> I am an individual requester. Under R.C. 149.43(B) I request copies of the following public
> records. I ask that they be provided electronically (PDF) at no charge where they exist in
> electronic form, per R.C. 149.43(B)(6)–(7).
>
> 1. **Ordinance 2026-42** (data-center moratorium, reported passed 2026-04-22) — the enacted
>    text as certified, including its title, all sections, its effective-date provision, and
>    any definitions section, together with the Clerk's certification.
>
> 2. **The recorded vote** on Ordinance 2026-42, including the roll call by member on the
>    ordinance itself and on the motion to suspend the rules for a third reading.
>
> 3. **Minutes and/or journal entries of City Council** for the meetings of **2026-03-17,
>    2026-04-07, and 2026-04-22**, in the form in which they were approved.
>
> 4. **Minutes of the Planning and Zoning Commission** for its meeting of **2026-04-09**, and
>    copies of the **two candidate data-center zoning codes** that body directed be drafted at
>    that meeting, in whatever draft form they presently exist.
>
> 5. **Pre-annexation agreements** executed by, or presently pending before, the City of
>    Findlay that cover any parcel in **Allen Township, Hancock County**, together with any
>    associated water-service or sewer-service agreement, for the period **2025-01-01 to the
>    date of this request**.
>
> 6. **Annexation ordinances 2025-62, 2025-63, 2025-65, and 2025-113** as enacted and
>    certified, including the petitions and legal descriptions filed with each.
>
> If any responsive record is withheld in whole or in part, please identify the record and cite
> the specific exemption relied upon, as R.C. 149.43(B)(1) requires. If any item above is
> ambiguous or is deemed overly broad, please contact me and give me the opportunity to revise
> it before denying it, as R.C. 149.43(B)(2) contemplates, and please tell me the manner in
> which your office maintains these records so that I can revise the request accordingly.
>
> Please acknowledge receipt. I am happy to take these on a rolling basis as they become
> available rather than waiting for a complete response.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> Cory Parent

### Constructive-denial watch

Ohio has no fixed statutory deadline; R.C. 149.43(B)(1) requires production "within a
reasonable period of time." An unreasonable delay is treated as a **constructive denial** and is
what makes a mandamus action under R.C. 149.43(C) available. The corpus already carries a worked
example of this posture — see `data/extracted/legal/prr-mandamus/` and the constructive-denial
item recorded in the sanitary-PRR batch.
