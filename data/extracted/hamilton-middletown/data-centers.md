# Hamilton–Middletown / Butler County, OH — Data-Center Activity Register

Discover-and-pin register for the Hamilton–Middletown watershed point — the lower **Great
Miami River** corridor. Status **as of 2026-07-02**. Tags are BOSC evidentiary discipline:
`[verified]` = cited public source (two+ independent or a primary instrument), `[reference]` =
single credible-media source, `[inference]`, `[open]`. **Nothing here is in the BOSC corpus
yet** — this records the *verified public record* and the specific primary instruments to
*pull*. Every figure is cited; none is fabricated.

## Disambiguation guardrail

The confirmed project sits in the **City of Trenton** (incorporated), Butler County, OH — not
the county seat (City of Hamilton) and **not** Hamilton County / Cincinnati. Trenton is ~30 mi
north of Cincinnati, in the lower Great Miami basin (HUC-8 05080002). `[verified]`

## 1 — Prologis "Project Mila" (Trenton data-center campus)

- **Developer / operator:** Prologis, Inc. (San Francisco industrial REIT). `[verified]`
  Source: Data Center Dynamics; Journal-News; WVXU; WCPO (all concur).
- **Project name:** Project Mila (developer's project name). `[verified]`
- **Anchor tenant / hyperscaler:** not named publicly. `[open]` — no end-user disclosed.
- **Developer shell / entity of record:** not disclosed in reviewed sources. `[open]` — pull
  the Butler County Recorder deed for the October 2025 conveyance to identify the grantee LLC.
- **Location:** Trenton Industrial Park, near Kennel Road and Woodsdale Road, ~1 mile south of
  downtown Trenton, east of the Molson Coors / Trenton brewery. Within Trenton city limits,
  zoned **I-G General Industrial** (no rezoning required). `[verified]` Source: WVXU; WCPO.
- **Land / parcel acreage:** ~141 acres. `[verified]` Source: Journal-News; DCD.
- **Buildings:** four single-story data-center buildings + an electrical substation + a utility
  building. `[verified]`
- **Square footage:** ~880,000–899,065 ft² at buildout (220,000 ft²/building → 880,000; the CRA
  tax filing cites 893,034 ft²; stopohiodatacenters cites 899,065 ft²). `[verified]` (range);
  exact total `[reference]`.
- **Investment:** ~$1 billion construction. `[reference]` (advocacy/trade press; not yet
  confirmed against a city/state filing).
- **Land purchase:** $7.7–7.75 million, Prologis → City of Trenton, October 2025. `[verified]`
  Source: WCPO; WVXU; Journal-News. (DCD's "$7.7M / Oct 2024" appears to be a date error.)
- **Assessed building value:** $233.2 million (basis $265/ft² × four buildings). `[reference]`
  Source: Journal-News (from the CRA filing).
- **Power draw (MW):** 250 MW. `[verified]` Source: Journal-News; stopohiodatacenters.
- **Power utility:** Duke Energy Ohio, Inc. `[verified]` — Duke's own transmission-project page
  describes two new 345-kV circuits (F34564/F34565) from the Woodsdale Substation to "a new
  customer facility located north of the substation" (the Prologis site sits directly north);
  substation expanded ~3.3 acres, work completion by December 2028. Prologis committed to pay
  100% of the power-infrastructure upgrade costs. Source: duke-energy.com Woodsdale project page.
  *Note: the advocacy site's "AEP Ohio" is contradicted by Duke's primary filing — Trenton is
  Duke Energy Ohio (#3542, PJM DEOK) territory. `[verified]`*
- **Jobs:** ~120 permanent full-time by December 31, 2035; ~$5M annual payroll (~$41,666/job).
  `[verified]` (media round to ~140). Construction jobs not quantified. `[open]`

### Financial / tax instruments

- **CRA abatement:** 15-year, 75% abatement on building value (Community Reinvestment Area).
  Taxable building value after abatement $58.3M; total taxable value incl. land $67.6M; projected
  ~$1.34M/yr to city + school districts. `[reference]` Source: Journal-News (CRA agreement).
- **PILOT payments:** Edgewood City Schools $940,200; Butler Tech $59,800. `[reference]`
- **Upfront infrastructure:** $2.47M water tap-in (617 ERUs); $210,400 sewer capacity (263 ERUs);
  ~$120,000/yr municipal water/sewer/stormwater fees. `[reference]` Source: Journal-News.
- **TIF / enterprise-zone / sales-tax exemption:** not separately documented (the structure is a
  CRA + PILOT, not a TIF). `[open]`

### Water / hydrology hook

- **Draw:** up to ~15–15.8 million gallons/year; Prologis claims water cooling needed ~3% of the
  year via direct evaporative free-air cooling. `[verified]` (draw range); cooling claim
  `[reference]` (developer assertion).
- **Water source:** City of Trenton municipal system — Trenton's own groundwater (four supply
  wells + a 2006 ion-exchange treatment plant); **not** purchased from City of Hamilton.
  `[verified]` Source: trentonoh.gov/239.
- **Wastewater:** Trenton gravity sewer → lift station → force main under the Great Miami River →
  Butler County's LeSourdsville Water Reclamation Facility (5260 Hamilton-Middletown Rd), under a
  Butler County Sewer Dept. contract. `[verified]` Source: trentonoh.gov/238; bcohio.gov.
- **Receiving water:** Great Miami River (LeSourdsville WRF discharges treated effluent to the
  Great Miami). `[verified]`
- **NPDES permit:** LeSourdsville facility ID 1PK00011 (Ohio EPA). `[reference]` — confirm the
  current NPDES number via Ohio EPA / EPA ECHO before citing as `[verified]`. No project-specific
  NPDES for Project Mila surfaced. `[open]`

### Hydrology screen

- **Receiving water (indirect):** Great Miami River, lower reach — the basin-screen mainstem
  proxy is gage 03274000 (Great Miami River at Hamilton OH; `watermark.hydrology.basin`).
  `[verified]`
- **Abstraction vs. flow:** ~15.8M gal/yr = 0.067 cfs average consumptive; against a lower Great

### Regulatory record (status as of 2026-07-02)

- **Site-plan approval:** Trenton Planning Commission, March 30, 2026 (~10-minute meeting, no
  public hearing — land pre-zoned I-G Industrial). `[verified]` Source: WVXU; WCPO.
- **Construction start:** fall 2026 (substation site work underway as of March 2026); first
  building ~2028, all four complete ~2029. `[verified]`/`[reference]`.
- **Zoning:** no rezoning action — I-G General Industrial; only a site-plan approval was required.
  `[verified]`
- **Stormwater:** stormwater-management system under technical review at approval; NPDES
  construction/stormwater permit implied but not documented. `[open]`
- **Ohio EPA air PTI (emergency generators):** `[open]` — Prologis states backup diesel generators
  run only for testing/emergencies; no Butler County PTI found in reviewed sources. Instrument to
  pull: OEPA air permits DB / eDoc, Butler County, entity "Prologis" or a shell LLC.

### Opposition / litigation

- **Organized opposition:** W.A.T.E.R. (Woodsdale and Trenton Environmental Resistance).
  `[verified]`
- **Moratorium:** denied — Butler County Commissioners lack authority over incorporated Trenton;
  a residents' moratorium petition was rejected on jurisdictional grounds. `[verified]`
- **Ballot effort:** signature drive for a proposed Ohio constitutional amendment to ban data
  centers >25 MW (statewide, targets ~413,000 signatures by July 1, 2026). `[reference]`
  (advocacy source — treat targets as a lead, not verified.)
- **Litigation:** none identified. `[open]`
