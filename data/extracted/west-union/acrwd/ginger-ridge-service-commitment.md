# Ginger Ridge — the water commitment to Buck Canyon, as the District recorded it

**Site:** west-union · **Epic:** #1983 · **Sources:** `data/documents/west-union/acrwd/minutes/`
(15 board minutes, 2024-01 → 2026-07), `../ginger-ridge/` (the executed NDA and the plan set)

This is the reconstruction of a single project from a public body's own meeting record, where the
customer is never named. Every quotation below is `[verified]` against the minutes cited; the
custody and content-verified dates are in the source
[`filename-map.yaml`](../../../documents/west-union/acrwd/minutes/filename-map.yaml).

## Why this file exists

Adams County is essentially unzoned. There is no rezoning, no conditional-use hearing, no council
vote — none of the legislative record that documents a campus at Sidney or Bowling Green. The only
local public body with something to decide was the water district, and the only continuous local
record of the project is its engineer's monthly report.

## Four things the record establishes

**1. The District designed for cooling water, in its own minutes.** "Supplemental cooling water"
is the engineer's phrase, on 2025-04-10 and again on 2025-05-13. It is the only cooling-purpose
water statement anywhere in this site's corpus, and it comes from the utility, not the operator.

**2. The one quantified figure is 100,000 gpd — and it is an interim figure.** `[verified]` that
the District's engineer said the temporary Ginger Ridge service must deliver 100,000 gallons per
day of potable *and* supplemental cooling water "until the large supply project can be completed."
⚠️ **It is not a disclosed campus demand and must never be presented as one.** The "large supply
project" — new wells, a treatment-plant expansion, a transmission main — is a separate and much
larger undertaking that this corpus has no figure for at all.

**3. The customer set the terms.** It wanted the design fast-tracked (2025-10-09); its team
reviewed and commented on a public utility's drawings (2025-11-12); it deleted a tank the District
preferred to keep (2025-12-11); and it took an easement where the District had held a purchase
option (2025-05-13). Recorded as sequence, not as impropriety — a funding customer directing scope
is ordinary. What is *not* ordinary is that the minutes cannot say who it is.

**4. The plan set names what the minutes cannot.** Sheet G-003's parcel table lists route parcel
**#30 as BUCK CANYON PROPERTIES, LLC**, Adams County auditor parcel **1830000070002** — the same
legal entity the U.S. Army Corps addressed its jurisdictional determination to
([`usace/west-union/`](../../../documents/usace/west-union/)). The drawing joins the pipe to the
federal file; the minutes never do.

## Carter Hollow, twice

The 2025-05-13 minutes put the campus transmission main's terminus at Carter Hollow. The USACE
NWP 39 verification permits **485 linear feet of fill in Carter Hollow**. Same hollow, two
agencies, two purposes, neither record mentioning the other. `[verified]` on both sides.

## What the record does not contain

- **Any agreement between the District and the customer.** No service agreement, no
  cost-participation agreement, no tap or capacity reservation. A 100,000 gpd commitment with a
  bespoke main and an unnamed counterparty leaves no contract in the corpus. `[open]`
- **Any Ginger Ridge line in the District's own capital budgets.** The 2024 and 2025 capital
  budgets carry no such item, and the FY2026 budget footnote reads "(Budget does not include
  construction projects financed with grant and/or loans)". Consistent with a customer-funded
  project outside the appropriation — but that is `[inference]`, and the agreement above is what
  would settle it.
- **The Ohio EPA plan approval.** Expected the week of 2026-07-13; not in the corpus. `[open]`
- **Roughly half the meetings.** 15 of ~31 monthly meetings between 2024-01 and 2026-07 are held
  here; all of Feb–Sep 2024 and Jan–Mar/Jun/Aug–Sep 2025 are absent. The two 2024 minutes present
  are the two carrying the project's origin, which is consistent with a responsive production
  rather than a full series — but the request scope is not in the corpus either.

## Naming discipline

⚠️ **"Project Galaxy" is not an identifier.** It also names data-center projects in Shelby County
(where press uses it for a campus Ohio EPA calls "Sidney Data Center Campus") and in Fayette
County (where it is Ohio EPA's actual facility name). Nothing establishes that any two of the
three are the same project. Join on instrument numbers, parcels and coordinates — here,
`LRH-2025-00457-OHR`, parcel `1830000070002`, and 38.646748 / -83.659828.

⚠️ **Ginger Ridge Road is not Ginger Lane.** Both appear in these minutes; Ginger Lane is an
unrelated seven-tap line extension.

⚠️ **Winchester Industrial Park is a different project** with a different engineer, different
funding and its own contracts. Its figures — $845,778 and $2,519,816 awarded 2026-07-09 — are not
campus figures.
