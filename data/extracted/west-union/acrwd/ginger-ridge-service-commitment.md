## Why this file exists

Adams County is essentially unzoned. There is no rezoning, no conditional-use hearing, no
council vote — none of the legislative record that documents a campus at Sidney or Bowling
Green. The only local public body with something to decide was the water district, and the only
continuous local record of the project is its engineer's monthly report.

## Four things the record establishes

**1. The District designed for cooling water, in its own minutes.** "Supplemental cooling water"
is the engineer's phrase, on 2025-04-10 (`M-2025-04` · p.2) and again on 2025-05-13
(`M-2025-05` · p.1). It is the only cooling-purpose water statement anywhere in this site's
corpus, and it comes from the utility, not the operator.

**2. The one quantified figure is 100,000 gpd — and it is an interim figure.** `[verified]` at
`M-2025-05` · p.1: the temporary Ginger Ridge service must deliver 100,000 gallons per day of
potable *and* supplemental cooling water "until the large supply project can be completed."
⚠️ **It is not a disclosed campus demand and must never be presented as one.** The "large supply
project" — new wells, a treatment-plant expansion, a transmission main (`M-2025-04` · p.2) — is a
separate and much larger undertaking that this corpus has no figure for at all.

**3. The customer set the terms.** It wanted the design fast-tracked (`M-2025-10` · p.1); its
team reviewed and commented on a public utility's drawings (`M-2025-11` · p.3); it deleted a tank
the District preferred to keep (`M-2025-12` · p.2); and it took an easement where the District
had held a purchase option (`M-2025-05` · p.2). Recorded as sequence, not as impropriety — a
funding customer directing scope is ordinary. What is *not* ordinary is that the minutes cannot
say who it is.

**4. The plan set names what the minutes cannot.** Sheet G-003's parcel table lists route parcel
**#30 as BUCK CANYON PROPERTIES, LLC**, Adams County auditor parcel **1830000070002**
(`data/documents/west-union/acrwd/ginger-ridge/Ginger Ridge Water Line Plan.pdf` · p.2, sheet
G-003) — the same legal entity the U.S. Army Corps addressed its jurisdictional determination to
(`data/documents/usace/west-union/Email Chain 1.pdf` · p.0 and p.3). The drawing joins the pipe to
the federal file; the minutes never do.

## Carter Hollow, twice

The 2025-05-13 minutes put the campus transmission main's terminus at Carter Hollow
(`M-2025-05` · p.1). The USACE NWP 39 verification permits **485 linear feet (0.129 acre) of fill
in Carter Hollow** (`data/documents/usace/west-union/Email Chain 11.pdf` · p.0, restated at
`Email Chain 12.pdf` · p.0). Same hollow, two agencies, two purposes, neither record mentioning
the other. `[verified]` on both sides.

The federal project description on the same page fixes the campus at **approximately 1,016 acres
north and east of Ginger Ridge Road and west of U.S. Route 52, at 38.646748 / -83.659828**
(`Email Chain 11.pdf` · p.0).

## What the record does not contain

- **Any agreement between the District and the customer.** No service agreement, no
  cost-participation agreement, no tap or capacity reservation. A 100,000 gpd commitment with a
  bespoke main and an unnamed counterparty leaves no contract in the corpus. `[open]` — lead
  `ACRWD-SERVICE-AGREEMENT` in [`leads.yaml`](../../../site/west-union/leads.yaml).
- **Any Ginger Ridge line in the District's own capital budgets.** No such item appears in
  `finance/Budget 2024 - Capital.pdf` · p.0 or `finance/Budget 2025 - Capital.pdf` · p.0, and the
  FY2026 budget footnote reads "(Budget does not include construction projects financed with
  grant and/or loans)" (`finance/2026 Budget.pdf` · p.0). Consistent with a customer-funded
  project outside the appropriation — but that is `[inference]`, and the agreement above is what
  would settle it.
- **The Ohio EPA plan approval.** Expected the week of 2026-07-13 (`M-2026-07` · p.5); not in the
  corpus. `[open]` — lead `ACRWD-OEPA-PLAN-APPROVAL`.
- **Roughly half the meetings.** 15 of ~31 monthly meetings between 2024-01 and 2026-07 are held
  here; all of Feb–Sep 2024 and Jan–Mar/Jun/Aug–Sep 2025 are absent
  ([`minutes/filename-map.yaml`](../../../documents/west-union/acrwd/minutes/filename-map.yaml),
  `meta.gap`). The two 2024 minutes present are the two carrying the project's origin, which is
  consistent with a responsive production rather than a full series — but the request scope is
  not in the corpus either.

## Naming discipline

⚠️ **"Project Galaxy" is not an identifier.** It also names data-center projects in Shelby County
(where press uses it for a campus Ohio EPA calls "Sidney Data Center Campus"; see
[`sidney/data-centers.md`](../../sidney/data-centers.md), "Disambiguation guardrail") and in
Fayette County, where it is Ohio EPA's own facility name on permits OH0151745 and OHGC17083
([`sidney/regulatory-watch.yaml`](../../sidney/regulatory-watch.yaml)). Nothing establishes that
any two of the three are the same project. Join on instrument numbers, parcels and coordinates —
here, `LRH-2025-00457-OHR`, parcel `1830000070002`, and 38.646748 / -83.659828.

⚠️ **Ginger Ridge Road is not Ginger Lane.** Both appear in these minutes; Ginger Lane is an
unrelated seven-tap line extension (`M-2024-10` · p.2).

⚠️ **Winchester Industrial Park is a different project** with a different engineer (Verdantas /
Keith Doll, against CHA / Lee Conkel on Ginger Ridge), different funding (ARC / EDA) and its own
contracts. Its figures — **$845,778.00** to EFI Solution for materials and **$2,519,816.00** to
B.P. Pipeline, LLC for the water line and booster, both awarded by Resolution 26-46 on 2026-07-09
(`M-2026-07` · p.0, resolution text at p.13) — **are not campus figures.**
