RE:	A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE ALLEN COUNTY SANITARY ENGINEER TO APPLY FOR, ACCEPT, AND ENTER INTO A WATER POLLUTION CONTROL LOAN FUND (WPCLF) ON BEHALF OF AN UNINCORPORATED AREA OF SHAWNEE TOWNSHIP, ALLEN COUNTY FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF WASTEWATER FACILITIES, AND DESIGNATING A DEDICATED REPAYTMENT SOURCE FOR THE LOAN:
­­­­­­
	The Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 26th day of February, 2026 with the following members present:  _____________, _____________ and ___________

	Commissioner   _____________   moved the adoption of the following:

WHEREAS, an unincorporated area Shawnee Township, Allen County, Ohio and more specifically properties along Shawnee Road and Hume Road have failing on-site septic systems which are contaminating the local steams and Ohio EPA is requiring elimination of the contamination source; and,

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners, Allen County seek to upgrade its existing wastewater facilities in the Shawnee Oaks subdivision and construct a sanitary sewer collection system and pump station to serve properties along Shawnee and Hume Road; and,

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners, Allen County intend to apply for Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) for the planning, design, and or construction of said wastewater facilities; and,

WHEREAS, The Ohio Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) requires the government authority to pass legislation for application of a loan and the execution of an agreement as well as designating a dedicated repayment source;

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio:

Section 1: The Allen County Sanitary Engineer be and is hereby authorized to apply for a WPCLF loan, sign all documents for and enter into a WPCLF with the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency and the Ohio Water Development Authority for the planning, design and or construction of wastewater facilities on behalf of the Board of Commissioners, Allen County; and,

Section 2: The dedicated source of repayment will be sewer revenue funds; and,

Section 3: This resolution shall take effect and be in full force from and after the earliest period allowed by law.
