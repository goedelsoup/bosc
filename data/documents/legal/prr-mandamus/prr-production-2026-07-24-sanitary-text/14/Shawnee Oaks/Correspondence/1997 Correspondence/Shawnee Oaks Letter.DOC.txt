Shawnee Oaks Sewer Customers

RE:  Shawnee Oaks Sewer Usage Fees

Dear Customer:

Please refer to the enclosed bill within this packet.  You will find that your sevice fee has been raised from $61.50 per quarter to $66.12 per quarter.  Cridersville treats the sewage from this area and bills our office.  Approximately 85% of the sewer service fees are paid to Cridersville for treatment leaving only 15% for maintenance and upkeep of the system by our department.

This is the fourth year of a five year period your sewer fees will increase to equalize payments with the rest of the Allen County Sewer District, which is now paying $70.74 per quarter.  The main reason for the increase is that along with Cridersville raising their rates, Allen County will be increasing maintenance on the sanitary sewer system, to reduce leakage from the aging sewer lines.  The County is also funding various projects within the system mandated by the state and federal government, of which sewer revenues are the sole source of income.
