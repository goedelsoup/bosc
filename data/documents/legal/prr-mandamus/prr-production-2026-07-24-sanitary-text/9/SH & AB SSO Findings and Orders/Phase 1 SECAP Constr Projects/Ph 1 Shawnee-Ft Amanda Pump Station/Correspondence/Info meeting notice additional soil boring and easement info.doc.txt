RE:  	Shawnee Sewer Sub-District
	Sanitary Sewer Overflow Remediation Program

Dear Property Owner,

If you have received this second letter, the County has identified your property as one in which an easement is necessary to complete the project.  The requested easements will be along existing road right-of-ways or existing easements.  The easements are necessary to complete soil borings and/or for construction purposes.  To keep project costs to a minimum, we ask property owners donate the easements to the County, but we will offer fair market value compensation.

Soil Borings are an important part of the design process as they provide subsurface information on the types of soil the contractor will encounter during the excavation process.  Soil Borings are obtained by removing a 4” core of soil vertically to the proposed depth of excavation.  The County and its contractors will be responsible to restore your property to an “as good or better” condition when the work is complete.

The County will authorize KOHLI & KALIHER ASSOCIATES, INC. to begin their field survey work to gather the necessary data for the easements during the month of April 2013.  It should be noted the work will not be continuous during this period.  The consultant may return several times to record different types of data as the project progresses.  Accordingly, we wish to advise that it will be necessary for crews to enter upon your property to obtain certain field data.  Section 163.03 of the Ohio Revised Code authorizes such entries but also require that reimbursement be made for any actual damage resulting from such work.  These crews have received strict instruction concerning the preservation of private property and public lands.  However, in the event that any valuable vegetation must be cleared in order to accomplish the work, you will be notified and informed as to the procedure to follow in preparing a claim for reimbursement.  In all cases, however, removal of vegetation as well as other damage will be held to a minimum.  If at any time you feel that our representatives have not given proper attention to private property, please notify KOHLI & KALIHER ASSOCIATES, INC. at once.

Allen County representatives will be at the information meeting to answer specific questions regarding the easements.  We strongly encourage you to attend, so that we can provide all pertinent information about the proposed project and necessary easements.

We sincerely appreciate your cooperation and assistance as we move forward with the project.  Should you have any questions please call 996-4670 Monday thru Friday from 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM.
