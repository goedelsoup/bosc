March 13, 2013

PRESS RELEASE

For Immediate Release:

The Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department is developing plans to upgrade the existing Shawnee II Wastewater Treatment Plant and existing Shawnee Sanitary Sewer Collection System.  These improvements are necessary to eliminate Sanitary Sewer Overflows (SSOs).  The SSOs discharge sewage into the Ottawa River during major rain events.  Allen County has been ordered by the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency (OEPA) to eliminate the remaining SSOs on the Shawnee Collection System by December 31, 2020.

The Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department will be hosting an informational meeting at Apollo Career Center, 3325 Shawnee Road at 6:00 PM on March 26, 2013 in an effort to communicate the details of the projects.

Stephen M. Kayatin, P.E.
Sanitary Engineer
