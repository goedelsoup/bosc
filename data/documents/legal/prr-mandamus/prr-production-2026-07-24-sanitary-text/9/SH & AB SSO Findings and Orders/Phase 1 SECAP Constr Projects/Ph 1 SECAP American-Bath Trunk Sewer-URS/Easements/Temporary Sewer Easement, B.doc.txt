KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS, that, for and in consideration of the sum of       One Dollar and 00/100 ($1.00) and other good and valuable consideration, The Vineyards of Lima, LTD., give, grant, bargain, and convey to the said BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, a temporary easement and right to enter upon the following described land for the purpose of constructing a sanitary sewer or sewers.

Said sanitary sewer or sewers shall be constructed substantially in accordance with the general plan thereof on file in the office of the grantee, THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, at the Allen County Courthouse, Lima, Ohio, and located approximately as shown on the map which is attached hereto and made a part hereof and marked for identification as Exhibit “B”.
	The within easement shall continue and remain in effect during the period required to install and construct the aforesaid sanitary sewer or sewers but in no event shall the same continue beyond the period of Twelve (12) months from the date of execution herein.
	The said BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO,  its agents, contractors, subcontractors, designee or anyone else acting on behalf of it, shall save the grantor, its successors and assigns harmless from any and all loss, cost, damage and expense from any cause arising out of any wrongful or negligent act of the said  BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, in connection with the construction of said sewer or sewers.
	As part of the consideration herein, grantees, by acceptance of this easement and right-of-way hereby agree that the Grantee performing any portion of this contract agrees to restore the land of the Grantors in substantially the same condition as existing at the time of execution of this instrument and to replace, if necessary, any lawn or hedge, fences, tile drains and/or driveways as well as the surface of all lands disturbed by reason of or in connection with construction of said sewer, in substantially the same condition in which the same were found at the time of commencing such work.
