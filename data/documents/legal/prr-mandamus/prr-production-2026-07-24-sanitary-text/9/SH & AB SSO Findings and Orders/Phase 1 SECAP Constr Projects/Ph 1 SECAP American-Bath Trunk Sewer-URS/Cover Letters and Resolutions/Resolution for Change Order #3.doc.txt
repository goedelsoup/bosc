RE:	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS Approve change order #3 with BEAVERDAM CONTRACTING, INC. FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE AMERICAN-BATH TRUNK SEWER REPLACEMENT PROJECT.

The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 30th day of June 2010, with the following members present: Greg Sneary, W. Dan Reiff and Sam Bassitt.

	Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

	WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners received and opened bids for the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project on February 11, 2009; and

	WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners authorized a contract with Beaverdam Contracting, Inc for the construction of the  American-Bath Trunk Sewer for a total amount of $993,973.30; and

	WHEREAS, by Resolution #525-09 dated July 8, 2009, the Board of Commissioners approved Change Order #1 resulting in a net increase to the contract of $126,187.60 and a new total contract of $1,120,160.90; and

	WHEREAS, by Resolution #926-09 dated December 23, 2009, the Board of Commissioners approved Change Order #2 resulting in a net increase to the contract of $40,450.00 and a new total contract of $1,160,610.90; and

	WHEREAS, the contractor experienced excessive ground water and poor soil conditions which caused extra work and dewatering of the site; and

	WHEREAS, the contractor, Beaverdam Contracting, Inc. has presented Change Order #3 to address the costs associated with the increase in trench width caused by the poor soil conditions and excessive ground water in the amount of $313,492.10, which would result in a new contract price of $1,474,103.00; and

	WHEREAS, said change order has been reviewed and accepted by the project engineer, URS Corporation and the Assistant Sanitary Engineer, Brad Niemeyer, is recommending and requesting that the Board approve the same; and

	WHEREAS, the Board deems this request to be in order and wishes to grant the same; now therefore

	BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO that the herein described Change Order #3 with Beaverdam Contracting, Inc. for the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project is hereby approved in the net amount of $313,492.10 for a new contract amount of One Million, Four Hundred Seventy Four Thousand, One Hundred Three Dollars and 00/100 ($1,474,103.00).
