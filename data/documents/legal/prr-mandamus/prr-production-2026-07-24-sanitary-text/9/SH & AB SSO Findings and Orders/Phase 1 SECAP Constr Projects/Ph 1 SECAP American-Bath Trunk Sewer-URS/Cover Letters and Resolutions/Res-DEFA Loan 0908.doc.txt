					                 September 30, 2008  Resolution #xxx-08

RE:	A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE PRESIDENT OF THE ALLEN COUNTY COMMISSIONERS TO APPLY FOR, ACCEPT, AND ENTER INTO A WATER POLLUTION CONTROL LOAN FUND AGREEMENT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF WASTE WATER FACILITIES; DESIGNATING A DEDICATED REPAYMENT SOURCE FOR THE LOAN.

		The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 30th day of September 2008, with the following members present: Greg Sneary, Sam Bassitt and W. Dan Reiff.

		Commissioner   moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

	WHEREAS, the Allen County Sanitary District seeks to constructs a trunk sewer line on Knollwood and Cole Streets to eliminate/reduce the risk of wet weather overflows to Pike Run; and

	WHEREAS; the Allen County Commissioners intend to apply for a Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) for the construction of said wastewater facilities; and

	WHEREAS; the Ohio Water Pollution Loan Control Fund requires the government authority to pass legislation for application of a loan and the execution of an agreement as well as designation of a dedicated repayment source; now therefore,

	BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO:

SECTION 1.	That the President of said Board be and is hereby authorized to apply for a WPCLF loan, sign all documents for and enter into a Water Pollution Control Loan Fund loan with the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency and the Ohio Water Development Authority for construction of sewer collection facilities on behalf of Allen County, Ohio; and

	SECTION 2.   That the dedicated source of repayment will be user fees; and

SECTION 3.  That this resolution shall take effect and be in force after the earliest
                         period allowed by law.

	Commissioner   seconded the resolution and upon the roll being called, the vote resulted as follows:  Mr. Sneary, ; Mr. Bassitt,  ; Mr. Reiff,  .

Adopted this 30th day
