RE:	Shawnee Trunk Design -SSO Elimination Project -	EPA – DEFA loan application

Dear Commissioners:

The Sanitary Engineer is engaged in the design phase for the construction of a new trunk sewer to eliminate a sanitary sewer overflows (SSO) in the Shawnee Waste Water Collection System as mandated by the Ohio EPA.  The Board approved a $577,700 engineering contract with URS for the same by Resolution #397-10 on July 8th.

A loan nomination form was submitted to and accepted by the EPA, Division of Environmental and Financial Assistance (DEFA), for funding of the project through an Ohio Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) loan.  We expect that the loan to be at 1% fixed, and though the term has not yet been determined, the anticipation is that this design loan will eventually be rolled into a loan to construct the project.

We’re ready to submit the actual loan application that requires a Commissioners’ Resolution authorizing the submission and designating the authorized signatures for the same.

I’m requesting that the Board authorize the submission of a loan application for a DEFA, WPCLF loan for this project and authorize the President of the Board to sign all related documents on behalf of the Board

Bruce Wells
Business Manager
