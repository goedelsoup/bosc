Answer:

The proposed joint location revisions shown on the attached drawings are structurally acceptable. In locations where the bars need to be bent due to the waterstop, it is acceptable to replace the key with an intentionally roughened surface for the full wall width or slab thickness. The roughened surface should have an amplitude of 1/4”. Where the reinforcing steel is to be “bent down”, these bends should be shop bent and not field bent.

Please note that the drawings submitted with this RFI are not the latest set of drawings.
