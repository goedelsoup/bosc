RE:	AUTHORIZE CLERK OF BOARD TO POST NOTICE TO BIDDERS AND ADVERTISE TO RECEIVE BIDS FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE AMERICAN-BATH TRUNK SEWER REPLACEMENT PROJECT, BID OPENING FEBRUARY 11, 2009, AT 10:00 A.M.

The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 14th day of January 2009, with the following members present:

	Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio applied for and received funding from the Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) in the amount of a one percent (1%) loan from the Department of Financial Assistance (DEFA) of the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency to help fund the $1.3 million dollar American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project; and

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners received written communication from Brad Niemeyer, Assistant Sanitary Engineer, seeking permission to advertise to receive bids for the construction of the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project; and

WHEREAS, the Board deemed this request to be in order and hereby grant same; now therefore

BE IT RESOLVED THAT THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, hereby authorize and direct the Clerk of Board to post notice to bidders on the public bulletin board in the Courthouse and advertise in The Lima News for three (3) consecutive weeks per Section 307.87 of the Ohio Revised Code, to receive bids for the construction of the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project.  A copy of the contract documents, including drawings and specifications are on file at the office of the Allen County Sanitary Engineer, 3230 N. Cole St., Lima, Ohio 45801.  A set may be obtained from the office of URS Corporation, 277 West Nationwide Boulevard, Columbus Ohio, 43215-2566 (1-614-464-4500) upon payment of $100.00 plus shipping cost for each set, make checks payable to URS Corporation; and be it further

RESOLVED, that bids in excess of $10,000.00 shall be accompanied by a bid guarantee and contract bond or certified check followed by contract bond to the Allen County Board of County Commissioners equal to bid not in excess of ten (10%) percent of the bid upon condition that if the bid is accepted, a contract will be entered within sixty days; and be it further

RESOLVED, the bids will be received on the above until 10:00 a.m., February 11, 2009, in the office of the Allen County Commissioners by the Clerk of the Board, Courthouse, Lima, Ohio, and will be opened and publicly read aloud by the owner or a representative thereof; and be if further

RESOLVED, that the Board reserves the right to reject any/or all bids received.
