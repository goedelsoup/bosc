RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, the Allen County Sanitary Department (hereinafter referred to as the “LGA”) has determined the need for construction of certain wastewater facilities as described in Exhibit A attached hereto and part hereof: and

WHEREAS, the LGA desires to obtain a loan from the Ohio Water Development Authority (hereinafter referred to as the “OWDA”) to finance construction costs of the Phase I Shawnee WWTP Project, Ottawa River Force Main Project, and the Fort Amanda Pump Station Project on the terms set forth in the Cooperative Agreement (defined below); and

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO:

SECTION 1.	That the LGA hereby approves the construction of the aforesaid improvements in cooperation with the OWDA under the provisions, terms and conditions set forth in the “Cooperative Agreement for State Planning Project” as set forth in Exhibit A (the “Cooperative Agreement”) and hereby authorizes the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Fiscal Officer of the LGA to execute the Cooperative Agreement with the OWDA substantially in the form set forth in Exhibit A.

SECTION 2.	That it is found and determined that all formal actions of this Board concerning and relating to the passage of this resolution were passed in an open meeting of this Board, and that all deliberations of this Board and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action, were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 3. That this resolution is hereby adopted and effective immediacy for Allen
County to protect public health and safety.

Exhibit A

Description of Design Activities to be Undertaken by LGA

Shawnee II WWTP Improvements Project

The project shall include improvements to an existing 2 MGD municipal wastewater treatment plant including a new 48” influent sewer, headworks building with screening and compaction, chemical feed, and influent pumping; a 1.2 million gallon concrete flow equalization basin with mixing system and accessories; a UV and post aeration building with flow monitoring, UV modules, rotary blowers, and diffusers; a non-potable water system; a 3,200 square foot operations building; electrical building, site work including demolition, dewatering, grading, buried utilities, fencing and gates, asphalt paving, and related improvements; SCADA system including instrumentation and control panels; generator and all related electrical improvements, plumbing systems, and HVAC systems. Bids will be on a single prime lump sum basis.

Ottawa River Force Main Project

The Project shall include the furnishing and installation of all materials, equipment, labor, and incidentals required for the installation of a sanitary sewer force main which includes approximately 8,932 L.F. of 20-inch pipe, including air/vacuum release manholes and related surface restorations for the construction in accordance with the Contract Documents. Bids will be on a unit price basis.

Fort Amanda Pump Station Project

The project shall include construction of a new wastewater pump station including, but not limited to: two-tier building with process area, restroom, and electrical room; five self-priming pumps; odor control; flow meter; site work including paving, grading, buried piping, and fencing; instrumentation including control panels, analytical instruments, and SCADA programming; electrical work including generator; plumbing systems; and HVAC sytems. Bids will be on a single prime lump sum basis.
