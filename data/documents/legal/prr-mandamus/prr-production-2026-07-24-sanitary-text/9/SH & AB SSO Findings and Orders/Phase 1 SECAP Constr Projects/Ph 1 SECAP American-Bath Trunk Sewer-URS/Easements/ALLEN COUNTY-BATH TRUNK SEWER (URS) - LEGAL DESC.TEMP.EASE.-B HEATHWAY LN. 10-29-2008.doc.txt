TEMPORARY EASEMENT “B”

20’ WIDE TEMPORARY EASEMENT
THE VINEYARDS OF LIMA, LTD. PROPERTY (Heathway Lane)

LEGAL DESCRIPTION

Being a 20 foot wide easement on land owned by The Vineyards of Lima, Ltd. (Deed Vol. 884, Page 498) in the Northwest quarter of Section 24, T-3-S, R-6-E, City of Lima, American Township, Allen County, Ohio.  The side lines of said 20 foot wide easement being 10 feet left of and 10 feet right of and parallel to the following described line:

Commencing at a monument box over a stone (found) at the Northwest corner of said Northwest quarter;

thence S 0°52’43”W, 251.63 feet, with the West line of said Northwest quarter (centerline of North Cole Street);

thence S 89°15’10”E, 220.60 feet, the aforesaid line passing through the East right-of-way line of North Cole Street at 40.00 feet;

thence S 88°51’05”E, 238.00 feet;

thence S 0°23’27”W, 10.00 feet to the PLACE OF BEGINNING;

thence continuing S 0°23’27”W, 109.51 feet to the POINT OF TERMINATION.  It is understood that the side lines of this easement shall be extended or shortened so as to meet at other easement lines.

The legal description is based on surveying work performed by Kohli & Kaliher Associates, Inc. in June and July 2008.  Bearings are based on the grid of the North zone of the Ohio State Plane Coordinate System.  Reference shall also be made to K&K Drawing Number B-471 for a graphic representation of this easement.
