 Request:  Please respond to the following gate questions:
    1. Gates SG-101B and SG-102B are shown on the “1P” drawings to have operators at elevation 806.33’.  Is there any reason why these operators cannot be mounted on top of the gate frames similar to gates SG-103A and SG-103B?
    2. Sheet GP-4 calls out gates 301 and 311 as “SG”.  Sheet 3P-2 calls out these gates as downward opening.  Please confirm that these gates are weir type downward opening gates.
    3. Sheet E-4 does not show any buckets in MCC-UV to feed the electric operated sluice gates SG-300 and SG-301.  Please clarify.
Requested By:	Greg Kemper

	PROJECT MANAGER

CC:  GAK/RFI File

Answer:

    1. Operator preference. 103A and B are down low because the manual screen will rarely be used - but operation of gates 101B and 102B will be occasional.

    2. Downward opening sluice gates are needed. We want the gate to be completely below the channel floor as the FRP weir acts as the effluent weir - not the gates.

    3. These gate operators will require that two 20 amp breakers (one for each gate) be added to the MCC-UV.
