 Request:
In coordinating with the City of Lima for the installation of the water meter set they had the following recommendation:
1. Maintain 1” service line but reduce the 1” meter to a ¾” meter due to the small water demand.  A 1” meter can produce 50 gpm, a ¾” meter can produce 30 gpm, which the city representative noted should be more than adequate.  Please advise what size meter set should be provided.
2. Please note that the City of Lima does not install water meters inside building structures anymore and require the meter to be installed inside a curb set.  They noted that this must be located outside of the fence line and they intend on installing as close to the fence line as possible.  The city of Lima will locate this.
3. Please also note that they require copper tubing be installed between the water main and the meter set as well as copper tubing out of the meter set towards the building.  Per addendum #4 Specification Section 40 1513-2.F (Domestic Water Supply) we had figured SDR 21 for the domestic water supply piping.  Do you want us to tie onto the copper piping outside of the curb set and stub into the building with SDR 21 or should we continue the copper all the way into the building?  The additional footage of copper to go full length would be approximately 40 LF, this would eliminate a splice in the waterline outside of the curb set.

  Requested By:	Greg Kemper

Answer:

    1. A ¾” meter is OK
    2. No comment
    3. IT is our preference that copper be run into the building from the meter.
