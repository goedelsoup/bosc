RE:	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS ACCEPT PROPOSAL FROM CLARK A. SPIELES, TO PROVIDE LANDSCAPING DESIGN AND INSTALLATION FOR THE FT. AMANDA ROAD PUMP STATION.

The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 15th day of September 2015, with the following members present:

	Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, William L. Horvath, Wastewater Collection Superintendent, has submitted written communication requesting the Board of County Commissioners accept proposal from Clark A. Spieles, for landscape design and installation for the Ft. Amanda Road Pump Station for a not to exceed amount of $44,980.00; and

WHEREAS, said contract shall be effective from September 15, 2015 through October 31, 2015; and

WHEREAS, the Board deems this request to be in order and in the best interest of Allen County; and

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO hereby accept proposal from Clark A. Spieles, for landscape design and installation for the Ft. Amanda Road Pump Station for a not to exceed amount of $44,980.00.
