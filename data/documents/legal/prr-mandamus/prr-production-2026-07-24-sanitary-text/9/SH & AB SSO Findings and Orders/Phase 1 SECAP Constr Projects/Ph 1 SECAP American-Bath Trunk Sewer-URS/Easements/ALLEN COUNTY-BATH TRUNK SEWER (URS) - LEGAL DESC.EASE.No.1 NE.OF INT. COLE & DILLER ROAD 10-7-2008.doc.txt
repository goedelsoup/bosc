EASEMENT No. 1

20’ WIDE SANITARY SEWER EASEMENT
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSINERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO PROPERTY

LEGAL DESCRIPTION

Being a 20 foot wide easement on land owned by Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio (Deed Vol. 789, Page 608) in the Northwest quarter of Section 13, T-3-S, R-6-E, American Township, Allen County, Ohio.  The side lines of said 20 foot wide easement being 10 feet left of and 10 feet right of and parallel to the following described line:

Commencing at a monument box over a stone (found) at the Southwest corner of said Northwest quarter;

thence N 0°45’33”E, 32.11 feet, with the West line of said Northwest quarter (centerline of North Cole Street);

thence N 88°45’00”E, 40.02 feet to the East line of an existing 20 foot wide Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio easement (Deed Vol. 501, Page 252), said point being the PLACE OF BEGINNING;

thence continuing N 88°45’00”E, 5.55 feet;

thence N 88°43’56”E, 393.00 feet;

thence N 46°43’59”E, 591.00 feet;

thence N 34°52’57”E, 800.00 feet;

thence N 56°16’38”E, 75.00 feet, the aforesaid line passing through the East line of the Southwest quarter of said Northwest quarter at 30.34 feet;

thence S 73°57’13”E, 67.00 feet to the POINT OF TERMINATION.  It is understood that the side lines of this easement shall be extended or shortened so as to meet at angle points and other easement lines.  The Easterly line of this 20 foot wide easement is contiguous with the Westerly line of an existing 20 foot wide Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio easement described in the first of two easements in said Deed Volume 501, Page 252.

The legal description is based on surveying work performed by Kohli & Kaliher Associates, Inc. in June and July 2008.  Bearings are based on the grid of the North zone of the Ohio State Plane Coordinate System.  Reference shall also be made to K&K Drawing Number B-469 for a graphic representation of this easement.
