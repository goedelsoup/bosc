RE:  Agreement for Professional Services Proposal from URS Corporation - Ohio.

Dear Commissioners:

In October 2007 the Commissioners entered into an agreement for professional engineering services in association with the Shawnee and American-Bath Inflow and Infiltration Remediation Projects and approved Work Orders Number One (1) and Number Two (2) of said agreement.  (Resolution #738-07 October 16, 2007)  Work Order Number One was for design services for the American – Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project and Work Order Number Two was for the development of a System Evaluation and Capacity Assurance Plan for Shawnee and American – Bath Sanitary Sewer Sub-Districts.

In March of 2009 the Commissioners approved Work Order Number Three (3) in association with said existing agreement for professional services with URS Corporation – Ohio.  (Resolution #143-09 March 11, 2009)  Work Order Number Three provided Construction Services in association with the American – Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project.  Said Construction Services included shop drawing review, assistance with contract administration, project close-out documents and other miscellaneous items for a fee not to exceed $69,600.

Please reference the attached resolution requesting the Board approve Work Order Number Four (4) in association with said existing agreement for professional services with URS Corporation – Ohio.  Work Order Number Four will provide preliminary design services for the Shawnee II WWTP upgrades and the Ottawa River Interceptor Sewer as well as final design services for the Ottawa River Interceptor Sewer.  For a complete list of work items covered by the Work Order, please reference Attachment A to Work Order Number Four.  The fees for Work Order Number Four will not exceed an amount of Five Hundred Seventy Seven Thousand, Seven Hundred Dollars, and no cents ($577,700.00).

If you have any questions, please contact me immediately.
