County Commissioners’ Office
		Allen County, Ohio
		February 19th, 2009  Res. #XXX-09

RE:	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS APPROVES WORK ORDER NUMBER THREE IN ASSOCIATION WITH AN EXISTING AGREEMENT WITH URS CORPORATION – OHIO FOR PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERING SERVICES FOR THE SHAWNEE AND AMERICAN-BATH INFLOW AND INFILTRATION REMEDIATION PROJECTS.

		The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 25th day of February 2009, with the following members present:  Greg Sneary, W. Dan Reiff and Sam Bassitt

		Commissioner Reiff moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

		WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #207-05 dated April 21, 2005, the Board of County Commissioners entered into an Ohio Environmental Protection Agency Findings and Orders agreement to eliminate unauthorized Sanitary Sewer Overflows (SSO’s) and prepare System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plans (SECAP) for both the American-Bath and Shawnee Collection Systems; and

		WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #738-07 dated October 16, 2007, the Board of County Commissioners entered into an agreement for Professional Engineering Services, as well as Work Order Number One (1) and Number Two (2) of said agreement, with URS Corporation - Ohio; and

		WHEREAS, Bradley T. Niemeyer, Assistant Sanitary Engineer, has submitted written communication requesting the Board of County Commissioners approve Work Order Number Three (3) (attached hereto) from subject agreement; and

		WHEREAS, Work Order Number Three (3) from subject agreement will provide Construction Services including shop drawing review, contract administration and project close-out documents during the construction of the American – Bath Trunk Sewer Project for an amount not to exceed Sixty Nine Thousand Six Hundred Dollars and 00/100 Cents ($69,600); and

		WHEREAS, the Board deemed this request to be in order and hereby grant same; now therefore

		BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, hereby approves Work Order Number Three (3) in association with an existing agreement with URS Corporation – Ohio for an amount not to exceed Sixty Nine Thousand Nine Hundred Dollars and 00/100 Cents ($69,600).

		Commissioner Bassitt seconded the resolution and upon the roll being called the vote resulted as follows:  Commissioner Sneary,    ; Commissioner Reiff,    ; Commissioner Bassitt,    .
