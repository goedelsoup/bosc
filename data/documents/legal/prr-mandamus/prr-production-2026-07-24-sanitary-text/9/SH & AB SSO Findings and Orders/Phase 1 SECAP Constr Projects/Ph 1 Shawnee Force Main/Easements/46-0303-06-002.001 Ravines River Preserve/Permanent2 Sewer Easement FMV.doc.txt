PERMANENT SANITARY SEWER EASEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS, that, for and in consideration of the sum of
Three Hundred Fifty Three Dollars and 85/100 ($353.85) and other good and valuable consideration, Ravines River Preserve, Ltd., the Grantor give, grant, bargain, and convey to the said BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, the Grantee a permanent easement and right-of-way, including perpetual right to enter upon real estate hereinafter described, at any time and construct, maintain, repair and replace an underground sanitary sewer or sewers and for all purposes incidental hereto, across, through and under the lands hereinafter described, together with the right to excavate and to refill ditches and/or trenches for the location of said sanitary sewer or sewers and the further right to remove trees, bushes, undergrowth, and other obstructions interfering with the location, construction and maintenance of said sanitary sewer or sewers.

Said sanitary sewer or sewers shall be constructed substantially in accordance with the general plan thereof on file in the office of the Grantee, THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, at the Allen County Courthouse, Lima, Ohio, and located approximately as shown on the map which is attached hereto and made a part hereof and marked for identification as Exhibit “A”.
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, shall have the right hereunder, at all times, to enter upon said premises by foot, vehicle or equipment to construct, to do all things necessary for the construction or reconstruction of said sanitary sewer or sewers, to maintain and to use said sanitary sewer or sewers in and across said premises, and to hold the Grantor(s) harmless from any and all loss, cost, damage and expense from any cause arising out of any wrongful or negligent act of the BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, in connection with the construction or reconstruction of said sewer or sewers.
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, shall have the right hereunder, at all times, to enter said premises for the inspection, excavate, repair construct crossing for ditches or creeks, temporarily remove fences, trim, cut, remove or control by other means trees, underbrush, and weeds, and remove all obstructions, buildings, structures or materials thereon for renewal of said sanitary sewer or sewers and for the doing of all things necessary to maintain said sanitary sewer or sewers in a proper condition.  The Grantors, their heirs, executors, administrators, successors and/or assigns shall not built, construct, or place on said above-described property any building, structure or other improvement.  Landscaping, fencing plantings or improvements of that nature are prohibited and may not be placed on said easement area.
As part of the consideration herein, Grantee by acceptance of this Deed of Easement, hereby agrees that the Grantee performing any portion of this contract will restore the easement premises to substantially the same condition as existing at the time of execution of this instrument and to replace, if necessary, any lawn or hedge, fence, tile drains and/or driveways as well as to restore the surface of all lands disturbed by reason of or in connection with the installation, maintenance, repair and/or removal of said sanitary sewer or sewers to substantially that condition in which the same were found at the time of commencing such work.
To have and to hold said easement and right-of-way unto the BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, and unto its successors and assigns forever.
And the Grantor does hereby covenant with THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, that it is lawfully seized and possessed of the real estate above described, and has a good and lawful right to convey it, or any part thereof, that it is free of all encumbrances, except __________________________________________________________________________, and that it will forever warrant and defend the title thereto against the lawful claims of all persons whomsoever _________________________________________________________, and as part of the consideration for this grant, the Grantor does hereby release any and all claims for damages from whatever cause incident to the exercise of the rights herein described.
