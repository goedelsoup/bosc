CONTRACT FOR RIGHT OF ENTRY

Parcel No.46100402005000, Shawnee Country Club, 1700 Shawnee Road, Lima OH 45805, hereinafter termed Owner and the Allen County Sanitary Engineer’s Department, hereinafter termed County, and the mutual promises of the Owner and County hereinafter contained agree upon the following terms for the entry by the County upon the above-mentioned real estate.

1.  Owner, on or after April 22, 2013, hereby grants to the County, its employees, agents, and other representatives the irrevocable right to enter upon for the purpose of obtaining soil borings necessary for the design of the Shawnee Sanitary Trunk Sewer and Pump Station/Force Main Project.  Access is restricted to the area of the proposed Pump Station.

2.  County agrees to return the property to as close as the original condition as possible.

3.  Owner hereby covenants with County that      he       is/are the true and lawful owner of the above described real estate and is/are lawfully seized of the same in fee simple and has/have good right and full power to grant this right-of-entry.

4. Owner will not charge County interest, rent or other compensations during the period of time County occupies the said real estate for the purposes aforesaid.

5.  Owner by granting this right-of-entry does not relinquish any rights whatsoever not contained herein.

6.  This right of entry will expire 3 months after signing.
