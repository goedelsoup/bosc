RE:	Allen County - Ottawa River Force Main Project
	Contract with S & S Directional Boring LTD

To whom it may concern:

Recently, your business was contacted by our engineer (AECOM) to determine the amount your company was due from our contractor, S &S Directional Boring LTD, in relationship to the above mentioned project.  After feedback from your business, a check was generated in the amount provided, and S & S Directional Boring LTD was asked to endorse the check making it payable to your business.

Allen County believes the enclosed check will pay for materials/services provided to S & S through Pay Request Number Three or approximately April 24th.  Your business may have provided materials/services to S & S during the last month of construction and S & S will soon be submitting Pay Request Number Four.

The Allen County Sanitary Engineer's Office prides itself in staying involved with our contracts and ensuring all sub-contractors and suppliers are paid by our contractors.  The process of generating a check for every sub-contractor and supplier for each pay request for these contracts, however, is time consuming and costly.  Our department would prefer to cut one check to the contractor for future pay requests.  To do so, your business is asked to sign the Partial Waiver of Lien for each of the future pay requests by our contractor.  Signing the Partial Waiver of Lien does not prevent your company from filing a lien in the future, it is only meant to state that a lien will not be filed against the County based on payments received.  If you have questions regarding the Partial Waiver of Lien, please contact your legal counsel or call my office (1-419-996-4670) or our engineer - AECOM (1-614-600-5854).

Thank you in advance for your anticipated cooperation.  If you have any questions, please contact our office immediately.
