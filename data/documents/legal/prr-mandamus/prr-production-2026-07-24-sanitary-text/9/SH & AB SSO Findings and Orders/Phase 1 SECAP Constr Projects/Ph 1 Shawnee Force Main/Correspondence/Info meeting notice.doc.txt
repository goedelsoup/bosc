	Sanitary Sewer Overflow Remediation Program

Dear Property Owner,

The Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department is developing plans to upgrade the existing Shawnee II Wastewater Treatment Plant and the existing Shawnee Sanitary Sewer Collection System.  These improvements are necessary to eliminate the Sanitary Sewer Overflows (SSOs) on the collection system.  The SSOs discharge sewage into the Ottawa River during major rain events.  Allen County has been ordered by the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency (OEPA) to eliminate the remaining SSOs on the Shawnee Collection System by December 31, 2020.

In an effort to communicate the details of the upcoming projects and how they may affect your property, the Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department will be hosting an informational meeting at Apollo Career Center, 3325 Shawnee Road at 6:00 PM on March 26, 2013.

Cooperation with our customers in the project area is needed for access to certain private property to obtain soil boring information necessary to complete the design for the proposed collection system improvements.  Approximately 25 easements from property owners along the proposed alignment will also be necessary to complete the construction of the project.  If your property has been designated for an easement/soil boring, you will find a second letter included with this mailing providing additional information and we strongly encourage you to attend the March 26th informational meeting.

We sincerely appreciate your cooperation and assistance through this process.  Should you have any questions please call 996-4670 Monday thru Friday from 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM.
