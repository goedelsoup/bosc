	The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 11th day of March 2009, with the following members present:

	Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

	WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners wishes to purchase permanent and temporary easements to construct, maintain, repair and replace sanitary sewers for the purpose of conveying untreated waste, and for all purposes incidental thereto, across, through, and under the lands hereinafter described below which includes 2 Permanent Easements and 3 Temporary easements (attached hereto); and

	WHEREAS, the property owner (The Vineyards of Lima, LTD.) has consented to convey to the Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio, their successors in office and assigns permanent and temporary easements on the  property for the total sum as listed herein.
