Dear Mr. Poffenbarger,

In accordance with Section V of Allen County’s Findings and Orders, Order number 1., E., iii on page 6 states, “The plan must be updated to describe any significant changes in proposed actions and/or the implementation schedule.  The plan must also be updated to reflect available information on the performance of measures that have been implemented.”  Please allow this letter to serve as an update to the proposed actions.

Allen County’s SECAP, as it was presented and approved in April 2009, details an American-Bath WWTP project should it be needed after the completion of the American-Bath Collection System Improvements Project (aka American-Bath Trunk Sewer Project).  As the proposed schedule in the SECAP indicates, Allen County was to monitor the performance of the American-Bath WWTP beginning at the completion of the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Project through August, 14, 2011.  The monitoring period was set to span several seasonal wet cycles to determine how the plant would respond to the increased flow rate from the larger trunk sewer.

We have experienced several challenging rain events during the monitoring period, but to date, the WWTP has been able to handle the resulting flows without violation or issue.  Now that the monitoring period has expired and the American-Bath WWTP has handled the flows from the newly constructed trunk sewer, Allen County believes no improvements are needed at this time to the existing WWTP.

In summary, Allen County does not plan to perform the American-Bath WWTP Improvements as detailed in the SECAP report submitted in 2009 due to the fact the project is not necessary to adequately handle the sanitary sewer flow from the newly constructed American-Bath Trunk Sewer Project.

If you have any questions, or require additional information, please do not hesitate to ask.
