County Commissioners’ Office
		Allen County, Ohio
		December 28th, 2012  Res. #XXX-12

RE:	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS APPROVES WORK ORDER NUMBER FIVE IN ASSOCIATION WITH AN EXISTING AGREEMENT WITH URS CORPORATION – OHIO FOR PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERING SERVICES FOR THE SHAWNEE AND AMERICAN-BATH INFLOW AND INFILTRATION REMEDIATION PROJECTS.

		The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 28th day of December 2012, with the following members present:  Greg Sneary, W. Dan Reiff and Sam Bassitt

		Commissioner Reiff moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

		WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #207-05 dated April 21, 2005, the Board of County Commissioners entered into an Ohio Environmental Protection Agency Findings and Orders agreement to eliminate unauthorized Sanitary Sewer Overflows (SSO’s) and prepare System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plans (SECAP) for both the American-Bath and Shawnee Collection Systems; and

		WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #738-07 dated October 16, 2007, the Board of County Commissioners entered into an agreement for Professional Engineering Services, as well as Work Order Number One (1) and Number Two (2) of said agreement, with URS Corporation - Ohio; and

		WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #143-09 dated March 11, 2009, the Board of County Commissioners Work Order Number Three (3) of said agreement, with URS Corporation – Ohio for Professional Construction Services during the construction phase of the American –Bath Trunk Sewer Project; and

		WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #397-10 dated July 8, 2010, the Board of County Commissioners Work Order Number Four (4) of said agreement, with URS Corporation – Ohio for Professional Engineering Services during the design phase of the Phase I Shawnee WWTP Upgrade; and

		WHEREAS, Bradley T. Niemeyer, Assistant Sanitary Engineer, has submitted written communication requesting the Board of County Commissioners approve Work Order Number Five (5) (attached hereto) from subject agreement; and

		WHEREAS, Work Order Number Five (5) from subject agreement will provide preliminary and final design services for the Phase I Shawnee II WWTP Upgrades for an amount not to exceed Six Hundred Forty Seven Thousand Dollars and 00/100 Cents ($647,000); and

		WHEREAS, the Board deemed this request to be in order and hereby grant same; now therefore

		BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, hereby approves Work Order Number Five (5) in association with an existing agreement with URS Corporation – Ohio for an amount not to exceed Six Hundred Forty Seven Thousand Dollars and 00/100 Cents ($647,000).

		Commissioner Bassitt seconded the resolution and upon the roll being called the vote resulted as follows:  Commissioner Sneary,    ; Commissioner Reiff,    ; Commissioner Bassitt,    .
