RE:  Agreement for Professional Services Proposal from URS Corporation - Ohio.

Dear Commissioners:

In October 2007 the Commissioners entered into an agreement for professional engineering services in association with the Shawnee and American-Bath Inflow and Infiltration Remediation Projects and approved Work Orders Number One (1) and Number Two (2) of said agreement.  Work Order Number One was for design services for the American – Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project and Work Order Number Two was for the development of a System Evaluation and Capacity Assurance Plan for Shawnee and American – Bath Sanitary Sewer Sub-Districts.

Please reference the attached resolution requesting the Board approve Work Order Number Three (3) in association with said existing agreement for professional services with URS Corporation – Ohio.  Work Order Number Three will provide Construction Services in association with the American – Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project.  Said Construction Services will cover shop drawing review, assistance with contract administration, project close-out documents, and other miscellaneous items outlined in Attachment A to Work Order Number Three.  The fees for Work Order Number Three will not exceed an amount of Sixty Nine Thousand, Six Hundred Dollars, and no cents ($69,600.00).

If you have any questions, please contact me immediately.
