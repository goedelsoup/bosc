RE:	American Bath SSO Elimination Project – Bath Trunk Sewer Construction
	EPA – DEFA loan application

Dear Commissioners:

The Sanitary Engineer is engaged in the design phase for the construction of a new trunk sewer to eliminate a sanitary sewer overflow (SSO) on the American Bath Sanity Sewer Collection System as mandated by the Ohio EPA.

A nomination form has been filed with and accepted by the EPA, Division of Environmental and Financial Assistance (DEFA), for funding of the project through an Ohio Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) loan at a 1% fixed rate for twenty years.  The project may go to bid yet this year and will require a formal loan application.

The loan application requires a Resolution by the Commissioners authorizing the application submission and signatures for the same through the.

I’m requesting that the Board authorize the submission of an application for a DEFA, WPCLF loan in the amount of $1.3 million for this project and authorization for the President of the Board to sign all related documents on behalf of the Board

Bruce Wells
