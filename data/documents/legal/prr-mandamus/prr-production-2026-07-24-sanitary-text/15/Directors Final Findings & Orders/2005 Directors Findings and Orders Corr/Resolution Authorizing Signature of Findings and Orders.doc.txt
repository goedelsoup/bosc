RE:	AUTHORIZE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO TO ENTER INTO AN OHIO ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY, DIRECTOR’S FINAL FINDINGS AND ORDERS.

The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 21st day of April 2005, with the following members present:

Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:
RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, the Shawnee Collection System, Allen County Sanitary Sewer District has six (6) unauthorized sanitary sewer overflows (sso’s); and

WHEREAS, the American-Bath Collection System, Allen County Sanitary Sewer District has one (1) unauthorized sanitary sewer overflow; and

WHEREAS, on April 7, 2005 the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio received Ohio Environmental Protection Agency (OEPA), Director’s Findings and Orders (attached hereto) from the Director of the OEPA to eliminate said sso’s, and prepare System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plans for the Shawnee and American-Bath Collection Systems; and

WHEREAS, the Director’s Findings and Orders requires the payment of a $49,226.00 civil penalty to the OEPA; and

WHEREAS, in lieu of payment of the $49,226.00 civil penalty, the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio will complete a Supplemental Environmental Project (SEP); and

WHEREAS, the OEPA’s approved SEP will be the construction of the Findlay Road Phase II Sewer Improvement Project, which includes the elimination of Whispering-Dixie Wastewater Treatment; and

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio have received written communication from Stephen M. Kayatin, Sanitary Engineer, recommending the board to enter into said Director’s Orders; now therefore

BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, hereby authorize the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio to enter into Director’s Final Findings and Orders of the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency, to eliminate the unauthorized sso’s, preparation of SECAPs for the Shawnee and American-Bath Collection Systems, and the construction of the Findlay Road Phase II Sanitary Sewer Improvement Project.

Commissioner __________________ seconded the resolution and upon the roll being called the vote resulted as follows:  Mr. Bassitt, _____; Mr. Sneary, _____; Mr. Reiff, _____.
