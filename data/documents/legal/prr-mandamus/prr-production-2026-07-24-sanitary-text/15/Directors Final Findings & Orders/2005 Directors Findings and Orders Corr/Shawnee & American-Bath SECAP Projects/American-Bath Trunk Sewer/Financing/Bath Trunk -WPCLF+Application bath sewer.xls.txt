PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The project involves construction of approximately 5,300 linear feet of 24" and 36" relief sewer to convey peak wet weather flows to the American Bath WWTP.  Currently three lines are coming into the trunk sewer on Knollwood Drive and during increased flow, causes overflows to be discharged into Pike Run and backup problems at S&P Apartments.  The project will eliminate/reduce these events byincreasing the capacity of the trunk sewer.  The project will positively impact water quality in Pike Run, a tributary of the Ottawa River.  The project will begin at the SSO location on Knollwood and continue west to Cole Street, turning north at Cole and proceeding to the Diller Road intersection where the alignment will turn northeast and continue parallel to the exising 12" sanitary line until reaching the existing connection at the WWTP.  The project will be constructed in the public road and/or road right of way and then transverse land already owned by the Commissioners who are requesting a construction loan at the hardship rate of 1% based on a County MHI $37,048.
