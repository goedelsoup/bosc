County Commissioners’ Office
		Allen County, Ohio
		October 16th, 2007  Res. #738-07

RE:	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS ENTERS INTO AN AGREEMENT FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES WITH URS CORPORATION - OHIO, FOR PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERING SERVICES IN ASSOCIATION WITH THE SHAWNEE AND AMERICAN-BATH INFLOW AND INFILTRATION REMEDIATION PROJECTS AND APPROVE WORK ORDERS NUMBER ONE AND TWO OF SAID AGREEMENT.

		The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 16th day of October 2007, with the following members present:  Greg Sneary, W. Dan Reiff and Sam Bassitt

		Commissioner Reiff moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

		WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #207-05 dated April 21, 2005, the Board of County Commissioners entered into an Ohio Environmental Protection Agency Findings and Orders agreement to eliminate unauthorized Sanitary Sewer Overflows (SSO’s) and prepare System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plans (SECAP) for both the American-Bath and Shawnee Collection Systems; and

		WHEREAS, Bradley T. Niemeyer, Assistant Sanitary Engineer, has submitted written communication requesting the Board of County Commissioners enter into an agreement (attached hereto) with URS Corporation, for professional engineering services in association with the Shawnee and American-Bath Inflow and Infiltration remediation projects; and

		WHEREAS, Work Order No. 1 from subject agreement will provide preliminary and final design for the replacement of an under-sized portion of trunk sewer in the American-Bath Sanitary Sewer Sub-District for an amount not to exceed One Hundred Twenty Five Thousand Six Hundred Dollars and 00/100 Cents ($125,600); and

		WHEREAS, Work Order No. 2 from above-mentioned agreement will develop a System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plan (SECAP) for both the American-Bath and Shawnee Collection Systems for an amount not to exceed Two Hundred Ninety Five Thousand Dollars and 00/100 Cents ($295,000); and

		WHEREAS, the Board deemed this request to be in order and hereby grant same; now therefore

		BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, hereby enters into an agreement for professional services with URS Corporation - Ohio for professional engineering services in association with the Shawnee and American-Bath Inflow and Infiltration Remediation Projects and approve Work Orders No. 1 and No. 2 for amounts not to exceed One Hundred Twenty Five Thousand Six Hundred Dollars and 00/100 Cents ($125,600) and Two Hundred Ninety Five Thousand Dollars and 00/100 cents ($295,000.00).

		Commissioner Bassitt seconded the resolution and upon the roll being called the vote resulted as follows:  Commissioner Sneary, Yes; Commissioner Reiff, Yes; Commissioner Bassitt, Yes.

Adopted this 16th	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
day of October 2007	ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO
