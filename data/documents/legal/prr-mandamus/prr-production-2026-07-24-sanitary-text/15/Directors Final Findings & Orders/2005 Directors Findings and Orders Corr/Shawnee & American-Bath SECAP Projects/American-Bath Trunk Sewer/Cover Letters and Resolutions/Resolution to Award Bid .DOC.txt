RE:	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS ACCEPT AND AWARD PROPOSAL FROM BEAVERDAM CONTRACTING, INC. FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE AMERICAN-BATH TRUNK SEWER REPLACEMENT PROJECT.

The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 11th day of March 2009, with the following members present:

	Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners received and opened bids for the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project on February 11, 2009, with the following bids being received:

Contractor	Bid Amount
Beaverdam Contracting, Inc.	$   993,973.30
Beheler Excavation, Inc.	$1,025,776.05
Degen Excavating, Inc.	$1,054,471.20
Stillion Brothers Excavating, Inc.	$1,089,233.00
Gleason Construction Co., Inc.	$1,167,102.10
Hume Supply, Inc.	$1,190,056.75
Anderzack-Pitzen Construction, Inc.	$1,217,000.50
Underground Utilities, Inc.	$1,229,433.40
Mark Schaffer Excavating & Trucking, Inc.	$1,254,000.00
Crestline Paving & Excavating Co., Inc.	$1,256,473.00
Fabrizi Trucking & Paving Co., Inc.	$1,546,775.00

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio and the Sanitary Engineer, Stephen M. Kayatin, have received the above bids and find all to meet the job specifications; and

WHEREAS, the low responsive bidder was Beaverdam Contracting, Inc., and upon reviewing, and tabulating their bid it was found to be in good order; and

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio received written communication from Brad Niemeyer, Assistant Sanitary Engineer, requesting completion of project work to be contracted between the Board of County Commissioners and Beaverdam Contracting, Inc., for the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project subject to the approval of a low interest Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) loan application administered by the Division of Environmental and Financial Assistance (DEFA) of the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency, for said project; and

WHEREAS, the Board deems this request to be in order and in the best interest of Allen County; and

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO hereby enter into a contract with Beaverdam Contracting, Inc., to provide completion of project work for the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project at a cost not to exceed Nine Hundred Ninety Three Thousand Nine Hundred Seventy Three Dollars and 30/100 ($993,973.30); and be it further

RESOLVED, the approval of said contract will be subsequent to the procurement of a low interest DEFA construction loan; and be it further

RESOLVED, that a fully executed contract be on file in the office of the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio

Commissioner __________________ seconded the resolution and upon the roll being called the vote resulted as follows:  Mr. Bassitt, _____; Mr. Reiff, _____; Mr. Sneary, _____;
