RE:	BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO ACCEPT AND SUBMIT A SYSTEM EVALUATION CAPACITY ASSURANCE PLAN TO THE OHIO ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY.

The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 29th day of April 2009, with the following members present:

Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, the Shawnee Collection System, Allen County Sanitary Sewer District has six (6) unauthorized sanitary sewer overflows (SSO’s); and

WHEREAS, the American-Bath Collection System, Allen County Sanitary Sewer District has one (1) unauthorized sanitary sewer overflow; and

WHEREAS, on April 7, 2005 the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio received Ohio Environmental Protection Agency (OEPA), Director’s Findings and Orders from the Director of the OEPA to eliminate said SSO’s, and prepare System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plans (SECAP) for the Shawnee and American-Bath Collection Systems; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to resolution #207-05 dated April 21, 2005 the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio entered into an Ohio Environmental Protection Agency, Director’s Findings and Orders agreement to close the County’s remaining SSO’s; and

WHEREAS, Section V. ORDERS, Number 2, Part D requires the Board to submit a SECAP for both the Shawnee and American-Bath collection systems by July 1st, 2009

WHEREAS, pursuant to resolution #738-07 dated October 16, 2007 the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio authorized a contract with URS Corporation – Ohio for professional engineering services to have prepared a System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plan (SECAP) for both the American-Bath and Shawnee Collection systems; and

WHEREAS, URS Corporation – Ohio has prepared said SECAP and submitted same to the Sanitary Engineer’s Department for review; and

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio have received written communication from Bradley T. Niemeyer, Assistant Sanitary Engineer, recommending the board accept the SECAP and authorize the submittal of the same to the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency; now therefore

BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, hereby accept the American-Bath and Shawnee Collection system’s SECAP as submitted by the URS Corporation – Ohio and authorize submittal of same to the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency in accordance with Section V. ORDERS, Number 2, Part D of the OEPA Director’s Findings and Orders.
