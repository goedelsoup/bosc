Information release

September 25, 2009

RE: Allen County American-Bath Trunk Sewer Project and the Cole Street Road Closure

Due to unforeseen excessive ground water conditions experienced during construction of the American-Bath Trunk Sewer Project, progress was brought to a halt and put the project behind schedule.

We believe the water issues are behind us and construction has resumed.  Progress is being made and pipe is being installed.  While we first expected the delays to move the opening of Cole Street to the end of October, revised construction estimates indicate that barring any future problems, Cole Street will be open to through traffic by mid-November.

Our office will provide an update on the status of the project progress in late October.
