RE:	SSO Administrative Orders Update

Dear Mr. Poffenbarger:

On April 22, 2005 the Board of Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio, signed the Director’s Final Findings and Orders in regards to the County’s Sanitary Sewer Overflows.  The following is an update as to the progress in accordance with the timelines outlined in the Orders.

In Section V of the Findings and Orders, Number 1. A., on page 5, the County was directed to advertise for bids, receive bids, and award contracts to complete the mainline and manhole replacement and/or rehabilitation in the Seriff Sub-basin Pilot I/I Remediation Project Area as soon as possible, but not later than January 31, 2005.

The advertisement for the mainline rehabilitation contract bids was placed April 16, & 24, 2004, and opened on May 4, 2004.  The contract was awarded and all work was completed by March 31, 2005.  Manhole rehabilitation work was completed by November 30, 2004.

In Section V of the Findings and Orders, Number 4, on page 8, the County was directed to post warning signs, twelve by eighteen inches (12" x 18") printed in at least one and on half inch (1 ½ ") letters, at each of the sanitary sewer overflows.  The Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department completed the task of posting the SSO Warning Signs on June 3, 2005.

Should you have any questions please contact our office.
