RE:	Director’s Final Findings and Orders – Elimination of Sanitary Sewer Overflows

Dear Commissioners:

This letter is being written as a request to place the attached legislation on the Board’s April 19, 2005 meeting agenda for discussion.  The resolution authorizes the Board of County Commissioners to enter into an Ohio Environmental Protection Agency, Director’s Final Findings and Orders.  The Orders require the commissioners to comply with specific compliance milestones (see attached summary) for the elimination of the unauthorized sanitary sewer overflows located in the Shawnee and American-Bath Collection Systems, and complete the construction of the Findlay Road Phase II Sewer Improvement Project, which includes the elimination of the Whispering-Dixie Wastewater Treatment Plant.  After the construction of the Shawnee and American-Bath Collection Systems during the early to mid 1970’s the department installed the unauthorized sanitary sewer overflows to eliminate the backup of sewage into customer’s basements.  The sanitary sewer overflows are an unapproved discharge, and need to be eliminated.  I would recommend the Board of County Commissioners enter into the Director’s Final Findings and Orders.

If you have any questions about the above information, please contact my office.
