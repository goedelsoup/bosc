We are in receipt of your letter dated September 3, 2009 in which you approve the System Evaluation and Capacity Assurance Plan (SECAP) as originally submitted on April 21, 2009 and subsequently modified on May 28, 2009 and July 24, 2009.  The approved SECAP outlines recommended improvements to control sanitary sewer overflows (SSOs) to a selected design storm.  The key items in the SECAP include 1) description of the improvements; 2) implementation schedule; and 3) financial and affordability analysis.  The recommended improvements are over $28 million dollars in cost and include new major trunk sewers for the American Bath and Shawnee II POTW’s and a major upgrade to the Shawnee II WWTP.  The detailed implementation schedules for these improvements have all design, bidding and construction tasks complete by December 15, 2015. The financial and affordability analysis in Section 11.3.5 indicates that the current 10-year projection can support the additional debt service associated with the SSO improvements. The projection uses historical revenue data for monthly service charges, new connection (tap) fees, future rate increases, and estimated new connections to determine future revenues over the next 10 years.  Likewise, the projection uses historical operation and maintenance costs and current and future debt service expense to determine expenses over the next 10 years.  The projection then compares anticipated expenses versus revenues to determine if an adequate amount of revenue surplus will be generated to pay for the new debt service expenses from the recommended improvements.

We recently reviewed the financial project prepared under Section 11.3.5 and our revenues for 2009 are significantly less than what was originally estimated. We attribute these down revenues to the economy and it is anticipated that this trend will continue in the near future.  The following is a detailed list of the revenue differences that we are seeing during review of the 2009 information:

    • Sewer service revenue is down $400,000 through the third quarter; it is anticipated to be down $600,000 for the entire year.

    • Connection fee revenue is down $150,000 through the third quarter; it is anticipated to be down $200,000 for the entire year.

    • Sewer service revenue from the new connections is down $40,000 through the third quarter; it is anticipated to be down $60,000 for the entire year.

    • Unemployment rates in the County have increased from 5.0% in 2001 to 10.2 in 2009

    •

This down revenue trend has affected the affordability of the recommended improvements in accordance with the detailed implementation schedule as previously submitted and approved.  It is obvious that the economic conditions in our County as well as throughout Ohio have dramatically changed over the last couple years. All municipalities and utility service providers are starting to see the impact in collection of revenues.

We have revised our financial projection to take into consideration the revenue information and anticipated shortfalls for 2009. We hereby request an extension to the original implementation schedules as approved with the SECAP.  We have prepared revised implementation schedules and they are attached for your review and consideration.

Please understand that the County is committed to closure of its SSOs at the earliest timeframe but only when it is affordable to the users of the system.  In order to demonstrate this commitment, the County will complete construction of the American Bath Trunk Sewer by the end of 2009, commence design of the Shawnee II POTW Trunk Sewer and WWTP upgrade in early 2010, and proceed with construction of the Shawnee II POTW Trunk Sewer in 2013 as originally planned.  The only improvement that has been delayed is construction of the Shawnee II POTW WWTP upgrade. Furthermore, the SSOs in both collection systems will be addressed by December 15, 2015 however, only one (1) SSO location will remain at the Shawnee POTW WWTP.

We appreciate your consideration of this request and please let us know if you need any additional information.
