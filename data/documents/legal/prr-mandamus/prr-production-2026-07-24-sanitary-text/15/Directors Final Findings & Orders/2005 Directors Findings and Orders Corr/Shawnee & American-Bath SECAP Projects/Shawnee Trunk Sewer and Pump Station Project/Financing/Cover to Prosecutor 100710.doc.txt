RE:	Design Loan Application – Allen County
	Shawnee Trunk Sewer Design

Under findings by the Ohio EPA, the Sanitary Engineering Department is engaged in a sanitary sewer project to eliminate overflows in the Shawnee collection system.

The Commissioners have authorized an application for funding of the design of an improved trunk sewer through the EPA’s Department of Environmental Financial Assistance (DEFA) and the Ohio Water Development Authority (OWDA) for a Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) loan.

The attached includes:

    • Four sets of the loan fund agreement for a $631,095.59 on a 1%, five year loan.
    • General Certificate signed by the Clerk of the Board relative to the transaction including a copy of the Resolution authorizing the application and minutes of the meeting at which the Resolution was adopted.
    • Opinion letter of counsel.

We’re asking for five signatures; Approved as to Form on each of the four loan agreements and one Prosecutor’s opinion letter.

With the signatures, we can finish the submission to the EPA in order to make their funding board meeting on October 28th.
