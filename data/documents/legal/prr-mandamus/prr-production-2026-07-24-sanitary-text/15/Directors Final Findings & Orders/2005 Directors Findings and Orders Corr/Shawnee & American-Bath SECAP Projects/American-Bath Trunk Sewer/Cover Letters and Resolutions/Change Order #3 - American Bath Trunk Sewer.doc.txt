RE:	American Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project
	Change Order #3

Dear Commissioners:

By Resolution #174-09 dated March 11, 2009, a contract was awarded to Beaverdam Contracting for construction of the Bath Trunk Sewer project in the amount of $993,973.30.

By Resolution #525-09 dated July 8, 2009, Change Order #1 was approved in the amount of $126,187.60 bringing the new contract price to $1,120,160.90.

By Resolution #926-09 dated December 23, 2009, Change Order #2 was approved in the amount of $40,450.00 bringing the new contract price to $1,160,610.90.

The trench width increased drastically during the installation of the sewer along Cole Street due to excessive ground water and poor soil conditions, which also caused delays and extra work.  The increased trench width also lead to additional aggregate and asphalt quantities to complete the work.  The contractor has requested, and the Engineer has reviewed, Change Order #3 to address the costs incurred from the delays as well as the extra aggregate and asphalt quantities.  The Change Order is for an increase to the project of $313,492.10.  The new contract price would become $1,474,103.00.

We would ask you approve the Contractor’s request.

If you have any questions or additional information is needed, please feel free to contact me.
