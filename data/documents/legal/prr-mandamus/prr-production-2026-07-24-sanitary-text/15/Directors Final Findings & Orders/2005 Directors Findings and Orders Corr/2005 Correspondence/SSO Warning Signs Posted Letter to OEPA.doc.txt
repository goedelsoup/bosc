RE:	SSO Warning Signs Posted

Dear Mr. Poffenbarger:

On April 21, 2005 the Allen County Board of Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio signed the Director’s Final Findings and Orders in regards to the County’s Sanitary Sewer Overflows.  In Section V of the Findings and Orders, on page 8, the county was directed to post warning signs, twelve by 18 inches (12" x 18") printed in at least one and on half inch (1 ½ ") letters, at each of the sanitary sewer overflows.  As outlined in Attachment B of the Findings and Orders the signs were to read as follows:

NOTICE
UNTREATED SEWAGE MAY OVERFLOW HERE DURING WET WEATHER. REPORTS OF SEWAGE DEBRIS IN THIS AREA SHOULD BE SUBMITTED TO THE ALLEN COUNTY SANITARY ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT AT (419) 331-6080.

This correspondence is to notify you that the Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department has posted the SSO Warning Signs as described within the Final Findings and Orders.

Should you have any questions please contact our office.
