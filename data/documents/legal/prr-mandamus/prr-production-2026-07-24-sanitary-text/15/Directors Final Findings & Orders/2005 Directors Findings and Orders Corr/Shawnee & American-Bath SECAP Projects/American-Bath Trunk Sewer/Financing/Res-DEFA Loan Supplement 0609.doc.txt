					                 June 24, 2009  Resolution #xxx-09

RE:	A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE PRESIDENT OF THE ALLEN COUNTY COMMISSIONERS TO amend a WATER POLLUTION CONTROL LOAN FUND AGREEMENT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF WASTE WATER FACILITIES.

		The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 24th day of June 2009, with the following members present: Greg Sneary, Sam Bassitt and W. Dan Reiff.

		Commissioner   moved the adoption of the following:

RESOLUTION

	WHEREAS, the Allen County Sanitary District is constructing a trunk sewer line on Knollwood and Cole Streets, referred to as the Bath Trunk Sewer Improvement Project, to eliminate/reduce the risk of wet weather overflows to Pike Run; and

	WHEREAS; the Allen County Commissioners applied for and were granted a Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) not to exceed $1,292,958 for the construction of said wastewater facilities; and

	WHEREAS; subsequent to the execution of the loan agreement, the County received an allocation of American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA) funds of $600,000 that will reduce the principal repayment amount of said loan; and

	WHEREAS; the WPCLF and Ohio EPA have requested that the County adopt a Supplement to said loan agreement to address the reduction in the loan amount, to access the allocation of funds and to include other stipulations required by the ARRA as addressed in the Supplement and associated exhibits, a copy of which is attached and made a part of this Resolution; now therefore,

	BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO: that the herein described and attached Supplement and Exhibits to the Water Pollution Control Loan Fund loan for the Bath Trunk Sewer Improvement is approved and be it further;

	RESOLVED; that the President of the Board is hereby authorized to sign documents associated with this action for said WPCLF loan on behalf of the Board.

	Commissioner   seconded the resolution and upon the roll being called, the vote resulted as follows:  Mr. Sneary, ; Mr. Bassitt,  ; Mr. Reiff,  .

Adopted this 24th day
