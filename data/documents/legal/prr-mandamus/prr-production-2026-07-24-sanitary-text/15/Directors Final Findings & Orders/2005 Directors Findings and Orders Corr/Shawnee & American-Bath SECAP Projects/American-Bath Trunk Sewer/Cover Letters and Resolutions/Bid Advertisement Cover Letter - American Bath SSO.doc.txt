Dear Commissioners:

Please reference the attached resolution requesting permission to advertise for bids for the subject project.  As you know, a loan from the Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) has been approved for the project.  This expected interest rate for the loan is one (1) percent for twenty (20) years.
