PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Allen County is requesting construction financing for activities associated with the elimination of a sanitary sewer overflow (SSO), tributary to its American Bath Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP).  The American Bath WWTP is located off Cole Street in Allen County, northwest of the City of Lima.  The project will allow Allen County to comply with Ohio EPA Finding and Orders issues in May 2005.
