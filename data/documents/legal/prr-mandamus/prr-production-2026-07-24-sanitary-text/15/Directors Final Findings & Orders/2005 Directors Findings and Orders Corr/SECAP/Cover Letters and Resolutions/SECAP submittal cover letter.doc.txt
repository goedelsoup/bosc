RE:	American-Bath and Shawnee Collection System Ohio Environmental Protection Agency   	(OEPA), Director’s Findings and Orders System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plan (SECAP) 	submittal

Dear Commissioners:

As you know, the Board entered into OEPA Director’s Findings and Orders in April 2005.  Part of those Findings and Orders required the Board to submit a SECAP for both of the above-mentioned collection systems.

The Board, in October 2007, entered into a contract with URS Corporation – Ohio to prepare said SECAP.  Over the last 18 months our department has worked closely with URS to get to the final draft of the SECAP.  We believe the report will meet the specification set forth by the OEPA in the Findings and Orders.  A draft of the SECAP was submitted to the OEPA last month, but they will not complete a detailed review until the Commissioner’s pass legislation accepting said report.

Please consider the attached resolution asking the Board to accept and submit to the OEPA, the final draft of the SECAP.  I will have a copy of the SECAP with me at the meeting, but have not sent a copy of the report as it is in two, 3 ring binders.

If you have any questions, or require additional information, please do not hesitate to ask.
