The low responsive bidder was Beaverdam Contracting, Inc.  It is my recommendation to award the contract to Beaverdam Contracting, Inc for a not to exceed contract price of $993,973.30.  The engineer’s estimate was $1,261,220.00.  The low bid was approximately 21% under the engineer’s estimate.

As you already know, we have applied for a low interest loan through the Water Pollution Control Loan Fund (WPCLF) administered through the OEPA.  The Division of Environmental and Financial Assistance (DEFA) will meet on March 26, 2009 to consider the requested loan for this project.  Our department has been working closely with the DEFA to provide all of the necessary components of the loan agreement package.  A resolution from the commissioners awarding the contract to the low bidder is one of the last necessary components.
