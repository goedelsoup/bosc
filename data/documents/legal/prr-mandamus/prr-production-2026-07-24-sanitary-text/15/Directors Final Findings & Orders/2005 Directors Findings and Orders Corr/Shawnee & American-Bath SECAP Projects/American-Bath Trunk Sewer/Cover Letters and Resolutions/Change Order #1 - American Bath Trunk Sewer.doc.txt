RE:	American Bath Trunk Sewer Replacement Project
	Change Order #1

Dear Commissioners:

By Resolution #174-09 dated March 11, 2009, a contract was awarded to Beaverdam Contracting for construction of the Bath Trunk Sewer project in the amount of $993,973.30.

At the inception of the project, a change in the design of the connection of the trunk sewer to the American Bath Wastewater Treatment Plant was reviewed and suggested by the Department.  A revised Permit to Install addressing the design change was requested and approved by the Ohio EPA.

The contractor has submitted, and the Engineer for the project has reviewed, Change Order #1 that addresses the design change.  The additions total $148,527.60, deletions total $22,340.00 for a net increase of $126,187.60.

Again, this change in design was at the Department’s request, not at the contractor’s initiation.  We would appreciate your approval of the same.

If you have any questions or additional information is needed, please feel free to contact me.
