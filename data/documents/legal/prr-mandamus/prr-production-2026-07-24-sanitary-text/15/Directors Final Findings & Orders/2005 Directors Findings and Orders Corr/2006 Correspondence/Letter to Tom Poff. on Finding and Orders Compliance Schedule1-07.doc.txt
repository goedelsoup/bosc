In accordance with Section V of the Findings and Orders, Order number 6 states, “Within fourteen (14) days of each deadline in Order 1, Order 2, and Order 4, Respondent shall submit written notification and documentation of compliance with said Orders to Ohio EPA Northwest District Office.”  Please allow this letter to serve notice for the December 31, 2006 deadlines as shown below.

Shawnee POTW

In Section V of the Findings and Orders, Number 1. B., on page 5, the County was directed to “Begin investigation and remediation of private property issues as soon as possible, but not later than December 31, 2006.”

In 2001, Allen County hired a consultant to conduct building inspections and smoke testing of private laterals for approximately 3,700 customers in the twelve designated sub-basins that comprise the Shawnee II Collection System.  The consultant was unable to complete nearly 350 building inspections for various reasons.  In 2005, the County completed any outstanding building inspections in the Seriff Sub-Basin and continues to address the balance of the outstanding non-inspects.

Also in 2005, the County completed the lateral investigations on all suspected laterals within two of the twelve Shawnee sub-basins. The aforementioned sub-basins are the Seriff Sub-Basin and the Shawnee Meadows Sub-Basin.  The county assembled a database of the findings and developed possible remediation methods to address building defects and the public and private lateral issues.  As a result, the County sent Notice of Violation Letters to property owners to address the building defects and also retained the services of a consulting engineer to develop bid specifications to address the defective laterals.  The lateral remediation project for the Seriff Sub-Basin will bid in the second quarter of 2007.
As a result of the aforementioned Notice of Violation Letters, the County was able to address approximately 50 known private property violations in the Seriff Sub-Basin.  The violations were in direct conflict with the Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department Rules, Regulations, Procedures and General Specifications Governing Sanitary Sewage and Water Supply and included illegal connections such as sump pumps, downspouts and area/foundation drains.

American-Bath POTW

Section V of the Findings and Orders, Number 2. A., on page 6, directs the County to “Complete American Bath POTW flow monitoring as soon as possible, but not later than December 31, 2006.”

In April of 2005, the County completed installation of six flow meters in strategic locations to generate flow data for the entire American Bath collection system.  The data gathered from these flow meters has been forwarded to a consulting engineer for evaluation and flow modeling to develop a solution for the closure of the one and only Sanitary Sewer Overflow on the American Bath Collection system.

In closing, upon receipt of this letter I would request you contact me to confirm that we have met our obligations as outlined in Section V, Numbers 1. B. and 2. A. of the Findings and Orders.
