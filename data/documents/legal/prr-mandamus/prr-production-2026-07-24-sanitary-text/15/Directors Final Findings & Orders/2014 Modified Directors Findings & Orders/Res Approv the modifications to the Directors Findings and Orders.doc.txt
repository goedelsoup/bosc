RE:	AUTHORIZE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO ACCEPT  THE MODIFICATION TO THE OHIO ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY, DIRECTOR’S FINAL FINDINGS AND ORDERS.

The Board of County Commissioners of Allen County, Ohio met in regular session on the 4th day of December 2013, with the following members present:

Commissioner ___________ moved the adoption of the following:
RESOLUTION

WHEREAS, the Shawnee Collection System, Allen County Sanitary Sewer District has six (6) unauthorized sanitary sewer overflows (SSOs); and

WHEREAS, the American-Bath Collection System, Allen County Sanitary Sewer District has one (1) unauthorized sanitary sewer overflow; and

WHEREAS, on April 7, 2005 the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio received Ohio Environmental Protection Agency (OEPA), Director’s Findings and Orders from the Director of the OEPA to eliminate said SSOs, and prepare System Evaluation Capacity Assurance Plans for the Shawnee and American-Bath Collection Systems; and

WHEREAS, the Director’s Findings and Orders requires the payment of a $49,226.00 civil penalty to the OEPA; and

WHEREAS, in lieu of payment of the $49,226.00 civil penalty, the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio will complete a Supplemental Environmental Project (SEP); and

WHEREAS, the OEPA’s approved SEP will be the construction of the Findlay Road Phase II Sewer Improvement Project, which includes the elimination of Whispering-Dixie Wastewater Treatment; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution #207-05 dated April 21, 2005, the Board of County Commissioners entered into an Ohio Environmental Protection Agency Director's Findings and Orders which mandated all work associated with closing the SSOs be complete by December 31, 2015; and

WHEREAS, the Sanitary Engineer has requested and received a five (5) year extension to the Director's Findings and Orders extending the deadline to December 31, 2020; and

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio have received written communication from Bradley T. Niemeyer, Assistant Sanitary Engineer, recommending the Board approve the modifications to the Director's Orders giving the County a five (5) year extension to close the remaining SSOs; now therefore

BE IT RESOLVED THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, ALLEN COUNTY, OHIO, hereby authorize the Board of County Commissioners, Allen County, Ohio to approve the modifications to the Director’s Final Findings and Orders of the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency, to eliminate the unauthorized SSOs.
