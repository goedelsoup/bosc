Hi Steve,

The modified FFO’s for Shawnee II were issued to the Allen County Commissioners.  The findings also state 
the WWTP and collection system are owned and operated by the commissioners.  Where storm water 
improvements are needed to address the I/I problems to the sanitary sewer and the WWTP, these 
improvements may be under the jurisdiction of Allen County. The NPDES Small MS4 general permit may 
influence how these improvements are conducted (eg. sediment and erosion control requirements, as well 
as possibly effecting the selection and sizing of a post construction BMP as per the NPDES Construction 
General Permit) and the review process ( require the development and review of a Storm Water Pollution 
Prevention Plan , SWP3), but the driving documents to conduct the improvements are the WWTP’s NPDES 
permit and the FFOs, not the NPDES Small MS4 general permit. There may be some areas (eg. township 
facilities) where Allen County does not have direct authority over the storm sewer system. In those cases, 
we encourage Allen County to work together with other storm water authorities and adjacent property 
owners to implement storm water BMPs. If the jurisdictional parties can’t reach an agreement, it remains 
Allen County’s responsibility to pursue any necessary improvements to the sanitary sewer to address I/I.

I hope this answers your question. If not,  please feel free to contact Peggy Christie or me.
