Steve, 
I think that the intent of the Strategy Plan in section (i) was to develop a plan to address I&I on the 
private system. The purchase the new camera truck is definitely a good first step toward identifying 
the laterals that have I&I. You would also need to incorporate a component to address the I&I. Your 
initial letter (Attached), which we considered acceptable,  indicated:

The Allen County Sanitary Engineering Department’s MOM manual shall be updated and 
revised to include strategies for detecting and remediating excessive I & I from private 
property service laterals.  Part of the department’s strategy is to develop a service lateral 
replacement reimbursement program promoting property owner repair of leaking service 
laterals.  The program budget will be established annually through the department’s budget 
process.  

The intent of section (ii) as you outlined it below seems acceptable. It would likely involve notification 
to the MS4 or political subdivision to address the storm water infrastructure improvements necessary 
to alleviate the flooding causing excessive I & I of storm water into the sanitary sewers.

Please let me know if you have any additional questions.
