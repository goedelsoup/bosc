John,

It was a pleasure to speak with you this morning regarding your existing 
Vaughan submersible pumps, model S6U-098 with 40 HP, 1750 RPM motors.  Your 
application is such that most of your TDH is static discharge lift, about 40 
feet plus or minus a couple feet depending on the wet well liquid level.
Since you have 12" discharge pipe and this run is so short (about 56 feet) I 
doubt that increasing the flow will have a large impact on friction loses.
Your engineer will need to check this out.

As we discussed, it is possible to put a 10.0" impeller in these pumps and use 
your existing 40 HP motor.  The first attachment below is a set of constant 
speed curves for the S6U pump.  Curve F is for the 10.0" impeller.
As you can see, 40 feet TDH is out near the end of the curve at about 2100 
GPM.  Operating the pump against less than 40 feet may result in high flow 
cavitation and is not recommended.  The second attachment is a set of variable 
speed curves for the 10.0" impeller.  Since you have VFD's on the pumps, my 
recommendation is to run them at about 1600 RPM.  This would result in an 
apporximate flow rate of 1900 GPM (at 40 feet).  An operational speed of 1650 
RPM would give you an estimated flow rate of 2000 GPM (at 40 feet).

The existing pumps are Serial No. 40274, sold in 1995 to American Bath WWTP, 
Lima, OH
