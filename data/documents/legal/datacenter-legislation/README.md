# Data-center legislation (original records)

**Collection:** `legal/datacenter-legislation/` · immutable source evidence

Verbatim bill text for Ohio legislation directed at **data centers and their
associated power generation** — the regulatory sibling of the corpus's data-center
record (parcels, permits, hydrology, grid) and of the `select-committee-2026/`
hearing record. Raw bytes are never edited; the reviewed reads and analysis live in
the mirrored
[`data/extracted/legal/datacenter-legislation/`](../../../extracted/legal/datacenter-legislation/).

## Contents

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `hb983_00_IN.pdf` | **H.B. No. 983, 136th G.A. — As Introduced** (21 pp; introduced 2026-07-28). The "Data Center Accountability and Citizen Protection Act." Enacts a new **R.C. Chapter 3744** — air/wastewater discharge standards for cooling systems (metals, glycols, PFAS), a **local citizen-vote** requirement for any new/expanded data center within 5 miles, **water-supply liability** with a rebuttable presumption, **grid-independence** (100% self-generation, no ratepayer cost), and a **local tax-incentive ban** — and amends **R.C. 9.66** to strip the confidentiality of data-center development-and-supply agreements. An emergency measure. Sponsors: Reps. Gross, Teska. |

enacted Act are separate [open] ingest targets. The strike/underline amendatory markup

PDF. The provision-by-provision read is in the
