# `sidney/cra-housing-council/` — the body that recommended the abatement

The City-Wide Community Reinvestment Area Housing Council was created by **Res. 84-22 §7** and it is
the body that, on **2025-10-23**, "unanimously resolved to recommend that City Council approve" the
Amazon CRA Agreement. Until this pull, the corpus held nothing from it and could not name a single
member.

## What these documents settle

**The 2025-10-23 minutes exist and were formally adopted.** Two instruments of this body say so
independently — the **February 5, 2026 agenda**, item 2, *"Approval of minutes: October 23, 2025"*,
and that meeting's **minutes**: *"A motion was made by R. Reiss to approve the October 23, 2025
meeting minutes; G. Snyder seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously upon vote."*

That converts an open question into a **targeted retrieval**. A records request can now name an
identified, approved, existing document — and the City cannot answer that no record was made.

**The absence is publication lag, not suppression.** No 2025 folder exists in either the Minutes or
the Agenda container, and the body's own May 29 agenda shows the **May 4, 2026** minutes are also
unpublished. That is the honest reading, and it is why a records request rather than a re-check of
the portal is the route.

**The Housing Council has no continuing oversight of this abatement.** On 2026-03-05 the Community
Development Director told the members that the Council reviews only 1- and 2-family housing
abatements, and that *"Industrial properties that have been granted an abatement will be reviewed by
the **Tax Incentive Review Council**"* for annual continuation. That body has **no container on this
portal at all**.
