# `sidney/planning-commission/` — Sidney City Planning Commission minutes

Two documents, and the pair is the point.

On **2025-06-16** the Commission was asked to recommend that Council zone the annexed 243 acres
**IIM**. A member moved it, another seconded, and the motion **failed with all four members present
voting no — the mover and the seconder among them.** The Community Development Director said on the
record that the request "will come before the Board again."

The **2025-07-21** minutes are the only Commission meeting between that night and the ordinance
Council passed anyway on 2025-07-28. Their full text carries `JOSLIN 0 · ANNEX 0 · establishment 0 ·
243 0 · Millcreek 0 · Vandemark 0`.

**A negative that consequential is held as a document, not asserted as a claim.** That is why the
July minutes are shelved at all.

The June minutes also carry the earliest primary-source public objection to this campus anywhere in
this corpus — **nineteen residents named with street addresses**, ten months before the Council
objections the corpus had called the first.
