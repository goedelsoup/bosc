## Two details worth keeping

**The graves.** Section 106 consultation (Ohio SHPO 2025-ADA-66354) documented three
cemeteries in or adjacent to the project area — the **Boone Family Cemetery** (OGS ID 85,
also recorded as Little #1, OGS ID 13191), the **Rogers Parr Cemetery**, and the previously
unrecorded **Davidson Cemetery** (OHI ADA0063512), found only because the survey boundary
expanded. Archaeological site 33AD0399 was largely destroyed; the Rogers Parr Cemetery within
it was largely intact. The Corps' permit conditions require temporary high-visibility fencing
at Boone and Davidson, **permanent** fencing around a 100-foot buffer at Rogers Parr, signage
at all three, and maintained pedestrian access after construction. The SHPO concurred in a
"no effect" determination on **Saturday 2026-02-14**.

**The bats.** The verification letter issued 2026-02-17 carried Special Condition 7 requiring
seasonal tree clearing. Ramboll objected the same afternoon — a 2025 summer **mist net
survey** was negative, and USFWS and ODNR had concurred that clearing could occur at any time
for five years — and a **revised** verification letter issued the next morning, 2026-02-18.
Twenty-two hours. The corpus previously carried only the 02-18 date; this file shows it is
the revision.
