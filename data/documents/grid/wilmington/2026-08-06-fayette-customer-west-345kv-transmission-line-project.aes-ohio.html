Fayette Customer West 345kV transmission line project

AES Ohio is proposing Fayette to Customer West 345kV Transmission line project , located in Jefferson Township, Fayette County, Ohio.

The proposed Fayette to Customer West 345kV Transmission Line Project  is a necessary upgrade to support 1.5 gigawatts of load growth in the Jeffersonville, Ohio area. Initial service is required by 2026, with additional transmission support by 2031. The proposed project involves the construction of two 345 kV transmission lines that will involve building two single-circuit parallel lines of approximately 0.3 miles and 0.2 miles (two parallel lines totaling approximately 0.5 miles), spanning from the existing Fayette Substation to a 345 kV delivery point.  The proposed Project involves necessary upgrades to serve a new data center customer near AES Ohio's Fayette Substation in Jeffersonville, Ohio.

The Fayette to Customer West 345 kV transmission line project is located west of Fent Road, southwest of Jeffersonville in Jefferson Township, Fayette County, Ohio.

During construction, customers should not notice interruption of their electric service. If an outage is required for a short period, the affected customer(s) will be notified before the outage to limit the inconvenience. 

Visit the OPSB website to view the application and for more information. The Case Number for the AES Ohio Fayette to Customer West 345 kV transmission line project is 25-0743-EL-BLN.

To contact AES Ohio about the project or request an electronic copy of the application, please leave a message at 937-331-4314 or email aesohiotransmissionmaintenancegroup@aes.com. You will receive a response within 1-2 business days.
