## The governance shape is the finding

A regional water district under R.C. Ch. 6119 is a public body whose board **is not
elected**. Under the 1994 by-laws its nine trustees serve three-year offsetting terms
composed "as per the Entry of the Court"; vacancies are filled by a three-trustee nominating
committee and a two-thirds vote **of the trustees themselves**. A tenth seat represents the
wholesale customers. Territory: the unincorporated areas of Adams *and Brown* counties, plus
incorporated areas of Adams County as the Common Pleas courts order.

The predecessor company's trustees *were* elected — by member petition and ballot at an
annual meeting, as the 1993 records show. The 1994 reorganization replaced that with court
appointment. Whatever the reason, the practical effect in 2024–26 is that the only local
public body in this project's path answers to a judge and to itself.

## ⚠️ Reading rules for this collection

1. **The customer is never named.** Across 15 board minutes the counterparty is "the
   company", "the client", "the Project Galaxy company", or "the company funding this
   project". The only document in the whole ACRWD production that carries the regulatory name
   **Buck Canyon** is the engineering plan set. No ACRWD document names Amazon or AWS.
2. **"Project Galaxy" is a codename in local use, not a key.** It collides with at least two
   other Ohio counties' records. See the parent [`README.md`](../README.md).
3. **Ginger Ridge Road ≠ Ginger Lane.** Both appear in the minutes; Ginger Lane is an
   unrelated seven-tap line extension. Do not merge them.
4. **The Winchester Industrial Park project is a different project.** It has its own
   engineer (Verdantas / Keith Doll, against CHA Consulting / Lee Conkel on Ginger Ridge), its
   own funding (ARC / EDA), and its own contracts. Its dollar figures are not campus figures.
5. **Digits from scans are not evidence.** The 2026 budget and the July 2026 minutes were
   read from rendered images. The July 2026 check register in particular OCRs badly
   (`Chris Baxla` → `Chrls Baxla`, `-24.65` → `-24,65`); take figures from the image.
6. **The financial statements are the District's own, marked "(In Process)".** The Auditor of
   State reports are the authoritative version and are not in this corpus.

## The one quantified demand

**100,000 gallons per day** — the 2025-05-13 minutes, describing what the interim Ginger
Ridge service must deliver in potable *and supplemental cooling* water before "the large
supply project" (new wells, plant expansion, transmission main) can be built. It is the
District's engineer's figure for a temporary arrangement, not a disclosed campus demand, and
it should never be presented as the campus's water requirement.
