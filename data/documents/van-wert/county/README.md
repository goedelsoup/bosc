# van-wert/county/ — Van Wert County Board of Commissioners record

The County's own published record for the Mega Site threads. Ingested for **#1407** (sub-issue of
#1267).

## What this collection is, and what it is not

The Board of Commissioners **publishes agendas and does not publish journal entries.** Every
action, resolution and vote of this Board is therefore invisible to this route — including the
2026-03-10 resolution granting the Mega Site annexation petition, which a City ordinance recites,
which is now corroborated by an agenda line, and which the corpus still does not hold.

So this collection establishes **that meetings happened and on what subjects**, never what was
decided. Read it as a calendar, not as a record of action.

The county website serves a 403 on `/commissioners/` and a 404 on `/commissioners/minutes`. The
working index is `/government/county_commissioners/<year>_agendas.php`, one page per year carrying
the whole year — which is why a single capture holds 63 meeting dates.

## What the 2026 agendas establish

**The negative:** zero entries across the whole year mention an enterprise zone, an abatement, a
Community Reinvestment Area, tax increment financing, or QTS by name. If the County has taken an
incentive action for this campus, it did not reach a published agenda in the first seven months of
2026.

**Four dated entries that matter:**

- **2026-01-29** — teleconference with Dan Baker, CCAO, "Re: General data center information".
- **2026-02-12** — "Brent Stevens, Executive Director, Van Wert Area Economic Development
  Corporation Re: **Megasite End User Options**". Three and a half months before the end user was
  publicly named, four weeks before the annexation petition was filed.
- **2026-03-10** — "Chuck Koch, Attorney, Koch & White Law Re: Annexation". Charles F. Koch is the
  agent for the annexation petitioners; this is the date Ordinance 26-05-028 recites for the
  Board's resolution granting the petition, and until now that date rested on the ordinance's
  recital alone.
- **2026-07-16** and **2026-07-28** — Ridge Township trustees, the County Engineer's Office and
  Van Wert City representatives on "Mendon Road and surrounding area expectations". The campus's
  address on its construction-stormwater NOI is 8002 Mendon Road (#1402).

Also on the record: a **2026-04-20 special session**, "Bus Trip to New Albany, OH Re: Data centers
(Commissioner Owens and Commissioner Lichtensteiger)" — corroborating the stakeholder tour already
recorded in `mega-site-instruments.yaml` from the City's side.
