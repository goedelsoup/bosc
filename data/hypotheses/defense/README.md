# H2 · Defense & Federal Enclave

Does the build-out track federal land — arsenals, air bases, federal research, the CHIPS
program? **Emerging hypothesis, under test.** A federal nexus is a *signal*, not a
verdict: most cells here are documented geography (`tag: verified`) whose causal link to
the boom remains inferred. Sites without a cell are *not yet assessed* — not cleared.

- `fields`: `nexus` (the federal/defense feature), `linkage` (how it relates),
  `capture` (the economic-capture reading), `federal_flow` (the dollars or jobs it rests on).
- `groups`: `arsenal` · `federal` · `supply` · `capture` · `watch`.
- Backing narrative: `docs/defense-nexus.md` (which holds the open question — the Lima
  campus's authorization posture, FedRAMP / DoD IL level, is undisclosed) and

## The capture sub-thesis

Added at ME-D (issue 1663). H2 used to be able to say only *where* the federal base
is, never *what its money does* — so "defense distorts the local economy" had to be filed
under H3 surveillance, whose `capital` field is about private operators and public subsidy,
not about federal payroll and prime awards. `capture` / `federal_flow` give the defense
reading its own economic vocabulary: the QCEW federal-ownership employment and wage share
(issue 1661), prime-award obligations resolved by verified UEI (issue 1662), and the
abatement / PILOT / public-benefit instruments — or federally-held acreage carrying assessed
value but no local tax duty — on the ground around the enclave.

The fields are independent of a cell's single `sub_thesis` frame, so a cell whose primary
frame is the arsenal nexus (Lima's) still states what the money does. The `capture` **group**
is for a site whose defense story is primarily economic rather than locational.

Note what the fields are *for*: Lima's read is a limit, not a confirmation — federal
employment there sits at a third of the national share, because a government-owned,
contractor-operated arsenal books its workforce as private manufacturing. A field that can
carry a negative is the point.
