**Dilution** is the mixing of a discharged pollutant load into the clean flow of the
receiving water. Because a stream is weakest at low flow, water-quality-based
[[effluent]] limits are sized against the design low flow — the [[7Q10]] — so the
discharge still meets criteria under the worst realistic dilution. Regulators may
recognize a bounded *mixing zone* immediately downstream of an outfall where
criteria are allowed to be met at the zone's edge rather than the pipe.

Dilution is the hinge between the water-quality thread and the data-center thread of
this record. A large [[consumptive cooling]] withdrawal removes flow from the same
reach that dilutes a downstream discharge, so it shrinks the [[assimilative capacity]]
available to every permitted load below the intake — a coupling the water-balance
model makes explicit rather than assuming away.
