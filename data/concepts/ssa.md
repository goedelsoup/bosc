A **sole source aquifer** is an aquifer that the [[EPA]] has designated as the
principal drinking-water source for its service area — one supplying at least half
the area's drinking water with no economically feasible alternative if it were
contaminated. The designation does not create a cleanup program, but it triggers EPA
review of federally funded projects that could contaminate the aquifer.

The label raises the stakes of any subsurface contamination in the designated area:
a release that would be a local matter over a redundant aquifer becomes a threat to
the region's only water supply. Where a sole-source aquifer underlies a facility
already inside a federal cleanup — a [[CERCLA]] site — the two designations
compound, and the groundwater pathway becomes the controlling exposure route in the
record.
