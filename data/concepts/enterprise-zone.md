An **enterprise zone** is an Ohio economic-development program in which a local
government designates an area and offers real- and personal-property tax exemptions
to firms that invest and create jobs there. The deal is struck in an *enterprise zone
agreement* that sets the exemption percentage and term against specific, enforceable
commitments — dollars invested, jobs created and retained, payroll thresholds.

The enterprise zone sits alongside the [[community reinvestment area]] as one of
Ohio's two main [[tax abatement]] frameworks; which one a project uses turns on
statutory eligibility and local practice. What makes the enterprise-zone agreement
useful to the record is its *clawback* structure: because the exemption is tied to
performance promises, the agreement states what the developer owes if the jobs or
investment fail to materialize — a test a capital-intensive but low-headcount data
center frequently strains.
