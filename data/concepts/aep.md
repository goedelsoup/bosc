**American Electric Power** is one of the largest electric utilities in the United
States; its Ohio operating company, AEP Ohio, is the distribution utility across much
of the territory this record covers. As a regulated utility it delivers power under
rates and terms set by the [[PUCO]], and its transmission sits inside the [[PJM]]
market.

AEP is a recurring named party in the data-center story because it is the utility that
must actually serve the new load — building substations, negotiating special
arrangements for hyperscale customers, and forecasting the demand growth those
customers drive. Its filings at the PUCO and its role in [[PJM]] planning are where the
utility's own account of a project's power needs appears, to be read against the
neutral baseline of the [[EIA]] utility data.
