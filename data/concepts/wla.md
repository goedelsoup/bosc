A **wasteload allocation** is the portion of a [[total maximum daily load]] handed
to point sources — the permitted dischargers, as distinct from the *load allocation*
given to diffuse runoff. It is the mechanism that turns a watershed-scale pollutant
budget into an enforceable limit at a specific pipe.

A state implements a WLA by writing it into the discharger's [[NPDES]] permit as a
water-quality-based [[effluent]] limit. So a WLA is where the abstract budget
becomes concrete: it determines whether a plant on an impaired reach has any load
headroom to absorb growth, or whether a new industrial or municipal load forces an
offset somewhere else in the watershed. When a reach's WLAs are already fully
subscribed, an added discharge has nowhere to go on paper.
