A **discharge monitoring report** is the periodic (usually monthly) filing in which
an [[NPDES]] permittee reports its measured [[effluent]] — flow, concentrations, and
loads — against each permit limit. It is a self-report, but a federally enforceable
one: a false or omitted DMR is itself a violation.

The DMR is the workhorse record of water compliance. Because the reports are public
and machine-readable through EPA's [[ECHO]], they let an outside analyst reconstruct
a facility's compliance history without a records request — recurring exceedances
are what tip a permittee into [[significant non-compliance]]. For the platform they
are a source of *reported* quantities: a DMR value is [verified] as filed, while any
figure derived from it (a load trend, an annual mass) is an [inference] built on top.
