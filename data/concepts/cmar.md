**Construction manager at risk** is a delivery method in which the owner hires a
construction manager early, during design, to advise on cost and constructability, and
the CM then commits to a *guaranteed maximum price* to build the project. The "at risk"
is literal: overruns above the GMP are the manager's exposure, not the owner's, which
aligns the CM with holding the budget.

CMAR matters to the platform because it shapes the cost record. Under this method the
early [[opinion of probable cost]] and the later GMP are the checkpoints where a public
project's price is set and can be reconstructed, and the CM is usually selected through
a qualifications-based [[request for qualifications]] rather than low bid. Knowing a
project used CMAR tells the analyst which documents carry the authoritative numbers and
how the contractor was chosen.
