title: Conditional use
kind: concept
aliases: [conditional-use permit, special use permit, special exception]
tags: [land-use, governance]
summary: A use allowed in a zoning district only by case-by-case approval, granted with conditions rather than by right.
related: [rezoning, variance, moratorium]
---

A **conditional use** is a use the zoning code lists as permissible in a district but
only with discretionary approval — the applicant must show it meets standards and
accept conditions attached to it. It is decided administratively or by a zoning board,
not by the legislature, which distinguishes it from a [[rezoning]]: the underlying
district does not change, the use is simply cleared within it.

The conditional-use path lets a project avoid a full rezoning fight when its zoning
already contemplates the use, trading a legislative vote for negotiated conditions —
buffers, hours, traffic or utility commitments. For the record, the value is in those
conditions: they are where a community extracts concrete obligations from a
data-center or industrial user, and where a later dispute over compliance is
anchored. A conditional use is a narrower instrument than a [[variance]], which waives
a standard rather than authorizing a listed use.
