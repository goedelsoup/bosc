The **Ohio Environmental Protection Agency** is the state agency that carries out most
environmental permitting and enforcement in Ohio under authority delegated by the
federal [[EPA]]. It issues the [[NPDES]] water-discharge permits, the
[[permit to install]] that precedes construction, and the air permits a facility
operates under, and it brings the enforcement actions when those permits are violated.

For this record the Ohio EPA is the agency whose files carry the operative facts about
a facility's water and air impact — the permit terms, the fact sheets, the compliance
history. It is also the agency a public-records request most often targets, and where
the tension between disclosure and a company's confidentiality claims plays out. Its
decisions sit alongside those of the [[Ohio Power Siting Board]] on the energy side and
the federal EPA above it.
