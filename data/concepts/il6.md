**Impact Level 6** is the highest tier in the Department of Defense's cloud security
model, authorizing workloads classified up to SECRET. Reaching it requires a facility
and its staff to meet stringent controls — physical separation, cleared personnel,
accreditation — well beyond a commercial data center's baseline.

IL6 is a signal in the siting record because it narrows who a facility can be for.
A campus built or accredited to IL6 is serving a government-secure mission, which
constrains the operator, the tenants, and often the secrecy around it — a stronger
confidentiality posture than a commercial [[hyperscale data center]] or a multi-tenant
[[colocation]] site. Where the record hints at an IL6 requirement, the platform treats
it as a lead about the customer and the purpose, held as an [inference] until an
accreditation or contract document confirms it.
