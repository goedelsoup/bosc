A **permit to install** is the [[Ohio EPA]] authorization a project must obtain
*before* it builds or modifies a source of pollution — a treatment unit, an outfall,
an air-emitting process. It is the pre-construction counterpart to an operating
permit: the PTI reviews the design, while an operating permit governs the source once
it runs — where applicable an [[NPDES]] permit for a water discharge, and for air a
Title V permit for a major source or a permit-to-install-and-operate (PTIO) for a
smaller one.

The PTI is often the earliest hard document in a facility's public trail, because it
predates operation. For a data-center campus, a permit-to-install application can
surface the on-site combustion, backup generation, or wastewater handling before the
operator has said anything publicly — which is why the platform treats a PTI as a
high-value discovery target alongside the [[construction general permit]] that covers
its stormwater during the build.
