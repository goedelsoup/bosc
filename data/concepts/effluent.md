**Effluent** is the treated water a facility discharges to a stream, river, or
lake. Its permitted *effluent limits* — numeric caps on concentration and mass load
for each regulated pollutant — are the operative terms of an [[NPDES]] permit, and
the permittee proves compliance by reporting measured effluent quality in the
monthly [[discharge monitoring report]].

Effluent limits are set two ways, and the stricter governs: technology-based limits
reflect what treatment can achieve, while water-quality-based limits protect the
receiving water given its [[dilution]] at design low flow. That second path is why a
[[WWTP]] discharge to a small stream can carry tighter limits than the same plant on
a large river — the same [[assimilative capacity]] logic that a new
[[consumptive cooling]] draw erodes by removing dilution flow.
