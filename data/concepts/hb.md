A **House Bill** is a legislative measure introduced in the Ohio House of
Representatives, referred to by number (HB 6, for example). Until enacted it is a
proposal; once passed by both chambers and signed, it amends the [[Ohio Revised Code]],
so a bill number is how a change in the law is tracked from introduction through its
codified result.

House bills matter to the record because the rules governing large projects are
actively being rewritten. Legislation reshaping utility and energy law, the siting
authority of the [[Ohio Power Siting Board]], or the property-tax tools behind a
data-center deal moves through numbered bills, often amid heavy lobbying. Following the
bill — its sponsors, its amendments, and the R.C. sections it alters — is how the
platform connects a statutory shift to the interests that pushed for it.
