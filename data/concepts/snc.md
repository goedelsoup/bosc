**Significant non-compliance** is EPA's threshold for a permit violation serious
enough to demand a formal response rather than a routine notice. For a water
permittee it is triggered by patterns in the [[discharge monitoring report]] —
effluent exceedances above defined magnitudes, or chronic reporting failures — not
by a single stray value.

An SNC flag is a signal worth following: it marks the facilities EPA itself has
sorted as problem dischargers, and it is visible through [[ECHO]] without any
records request. An unresolved SNC is frequently the on-ramp to a formal enforcement
action and, ultimately, a [[consent decree]]. The platform reads an SNC designation
as [verified] when it appears in the ECHO record, and treats the underlying cause as
a lead to trace back through the [[NPDES]] permit and its monitoring history.
