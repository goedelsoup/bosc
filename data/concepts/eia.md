The **Energy Information Administration** is the independent statistical arm of the
U.S. Department of Energy. It publishes the authoritative public numbers on energy —
generation, fuel mix, prices, and, through survey forms like EIA-861, the sales and
customer counts of individual electric utilities.

EIA data is the platform's neutral baseline for the energy backdrop of a site: it
establishes a utility's existing load and customer base as [reference] facts, against
which the *demand pressure* of a new hyperscale campus can be measured. Because it is
survey-based and utility-level, EIA is how an outside analyst estimates what share of
a utility's growth a single data center represents — a framing that a company like
[[AEP]] or the [[PJM]] load forecast can then corroborate or complicate.
