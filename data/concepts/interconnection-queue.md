The **interconnection queue** is the ordered backlog of projects — new generators and,
increasingly, large loads like data centers — waiting for the [[regional transmission
organization]] to study their effect on the grid and approve their connection. Each
entrant holds a dated queue position, and its studies determine what network upgrades
it must fund before it can energize.

The queue is one of the most reliable early indicators of a hyperscale project. A
campus that has said nothing publicly may already sit in [[PJM]]'s queue with a load
figure and a point of interconnection attached, and the studies expose the upgrade
cost and timeline the local grid faces. A long queue or a costly upgrade is also what
pushes an operator toward [[behind-the-meter]] generation to sidestep the wait — so
the queue shapes not just *when* a project connects but *how* it chooses to power
itself.
