The **Public Utilities Commission of Ohio** regulates the state's investor-owned
utilities — setting the rates, terms, and service standards under which a company
like [[AEP]] Ohio delivers electricity. Its cases run as docketed proceedings with
filed testimony and public comment, so a PUCO docket is a durable record of what a
utility proposed and what it was allowed.

PUCO is where the *cost* of accommodating a new large load can surface: a utility's
request to build infrastructure, recover a special-contract arrangement, or shift the
cost of serving a hyperscale customer onto or away from other ratepayers runs through
the commission. It pairs with the [[Ohio Power Siting Board]], which certifies where a
facility is built, and sits under the federal [[FERC]] for anything touching
interstate wholesale power — a three-way division the platform keeps straight when
tracing who approved what.
