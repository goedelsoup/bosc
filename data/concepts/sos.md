The **Secretary of State** is the state office that registers business entities and
keeps their public filings — articles of organization, statutory agents, annual
reports, and the record of mergers and name changes. For an investigator it is the
entry point of corporate genealogy: the place a limited-liability company's paper trail
begins.

The SOS record is central to the entity work in this platform because data-center
developers routinely route a project through single-purpose LLCs with anodyne names.
Chasing the filings — the registered agent shared across shells, the formation date
that lines up with a land option, the parent disclosed in an out-of-state filing — is
how the platform pierces a code name toward the operator behind it. The trail is often
partial by design, which is where a [[non-disclosure agreement]] and other
confidentiality layers pick up where the public filing goes quiet.
