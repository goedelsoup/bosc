The **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency** is the federal environmental regulator.
It writes the national rules under statutes like the Clean Water Act and CERCLA, runs
the public data systems the platform relies on — [[ECHO]], the [[TRI]], and the
[[RSEI]] model built on it — and retains direct authority over federal programs and
facilities.

Much of the day-to-day permitting and enforcement, though, is *delegated*: the EPA
sets the floor and a state agency like the [[Ohio EPA]] issues the permits and brings
most of the cases. That division is why a facility's record usually has both a federal
and a state layer, and why the platform reads EPA data as the neutral national
baseline while treating a state agency's actions as the operative local decisions.
