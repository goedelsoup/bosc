A **construction general permit** is the [[NPDES]] mechanism for stormwater running
off a construction site. Rather than issue an individual permit to every project, the
regulator writes one general permit and a site enrolls under it by filing a notice of
intent and a stormwater pollution prevention plan. It applies to construction
disturbing an acre or more — the threshold most large campus builds clear easily.

The CGP is the earliest water-side paper trail of a project's *construction* phase,
distinct from the operating [[NPDES]] permit that governs the finished facility. Its
enrollment names the operator, the acreage disturbed, and the receiving water, and it
obligates the [[erosion and sediment control]] practices that keep bare-soil runoff —
amplified by the site's soils and [[curve number]] — out of the stream during the
build. For the platform, a CGP filing is often the first hard confirmation that a
proposed data-center site is actually moving dirt.
