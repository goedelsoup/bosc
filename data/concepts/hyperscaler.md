A **hyperscaler** is one of the handful of companies that operate cloud and computing
infrastructure at the largest scale — Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and Meta foremost among
them. They design, own, and run their [[hyperscale data center]] campuses directly, as
opposed to renting capacity in a multi-tenant [[colocation]] facility.

The hyperscaler is the actor at the center of the siting story, and usually the hardest
to see. Behind a code-named LLC and a [[non-disclosure agreement]], the same few
operators are driving the land options, the power demand that fills the
[[interconnection queue]] or forces [[behind-the-meter]] generation, and the water draw
a community is asked to accommodate. Identifying which hyperscaler stands behind a
project — from its power profile, its secure-tier requirements, its known expansion
pattern — is often the pivotal [inference] the platform works to establish and then
confirm.
