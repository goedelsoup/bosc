A **wastewater treatment plant** collects, treats, and discharges a community's
sewage. A municipally owned plant is a [[POTW]] in Clean Water Act terms; some
operators brand a modernized plant a *water reclamation facility* (WRF) to signal
higher-grade treatment and reuse. Either way, its treated [[effluent]] leaves
through a permitted outfall governed by an [[NPDES]] permit and is logged in the
plant's monthly [[discharge monitoring report]].

A WWTP is a load *and* a dilution source in the water balance: it adds treated flow
to the receiving water, so its discharge is part of the low-flow denominator that a
new [[consumptive cooling]] withdrawal competes against. When a data-center or
industrial user is added upstream, the plant's headroom — hydraulic and permitted —
is one of the first constraints the record tests.
