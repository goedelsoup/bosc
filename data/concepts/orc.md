The **Ohio Revised Code** is the compiled, numbered body of Ohio's statutes, cited in
the form *R.C. 149.43* (or ORC 149.43). It is the legal backbone of the record: nearly
every authority a party invokes — an agency's duty, a tool it uses, a right the
requester asserts — traces to a specific section of the code.

The same citations recur across the platform's threads. R.C. 149.43 is the Public
Records Act behind a [[constructive denial]] and a [[public-records mandamus]];
R.C. 3735 authorizes the [[community reinvestment area]]; R.C. 715.70 the
[[joint economic development district]]; R.C. 6111 the state water-pollution program.
A section number changes only when the legislature amends it through a
[[House Bill]] — so pinning a claim to its exact R.C. citation is how the platform ties
an action to the precise authority in force when it was taken.
