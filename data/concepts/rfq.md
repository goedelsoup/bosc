A **request for qualifications** is a public solicitation that asks interested firms to
document their experience, staffing, and capacity — their *qualifications* — rather than
a price. An agency uses it to shortlist candidates before a priced proposal, or, for
professional and qualifications-based services, to select outright and negotiate the fee
afterward.

The RFQ is the front door to a project's procurement record and often names the field of
competitors before anyone is chosen. It is the customary selection route for a
[[construction manager at risk]] and for design engineers, where qualifications rather
than low bid govern. It pairs with the [[request for proposals]], which does ask for a
scoped, priced offer — together they mark how and on what basis a public project's team
was assembled.
