A **request for proposals** is a public solicitation that asks firms for a complete,
scoped, and priced offer to do the work. Unlike a pure low-bid invitation, an RFP is
scored on stated criteria — technical approach, schedule, team, and price together — so
the agency can select the best value rather than merely the cheapest number.

For the platform, an RFP and the proposals it draws are a rich seam of the public
record: they lay out how a project was defined, who competed, and what each offered.
An RFP typically follows a [[request for qualifications]] when an agency first
shortlists on competence and then seeks priced proposals from the shortlist, and its
figures can be read against the design-phase [[opinion of probable cost]] to see how
bid reality tracked the engineer's estimate.
