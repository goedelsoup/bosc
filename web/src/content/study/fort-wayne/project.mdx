The land is public, so start there. Eleven contiguous parcels in southeast Fort Wayne — along
Tillman and Adams Center Roads — all name the same owner of record: **Hatchworks LLC**. Read the
deeds as a sequence rather than a snapshot and the assemblage tells you it was deliberate: four
parcels in **January 2024**, around the April 2024 $2B groundbreaking, and **seven more on a
single day, 2025-10-31**, the Phase 3 expansion. One buyer, one purpose, bought quietly enough
that the operator's name appears nowhere on the deeds. `[verified]`

## The shell, and the tell

Hatchworks LLC is a **Delaware** entity, and ten of the eleven parcels list the owner's mailing
address as 2801 Centerville Road, Wilmington, DE 19808 — a registered-agent address, the kind a
company uses precisely so the deed doesn't say who it is. `[verified]`

One parcel breaks the pattern. The situs anchor, **6015 Adams Center Road**, mails its tax notices
to Mountain View, California 94043. `[inference]` A single clerical inconsistency in a
property-tax field is the first thread connecting the Delaware shell to its operator — and on its
own it is exactly that, a thread, not a finding.

## The caption that says the quiet part

The confirmation comes from a different agency. When Hatchworks applied to IDEM for a §401
water-quality certification to expand the campus,
<Cite record="idem/fort-wayne/wqc001454.idem.yaml">the administrative record stopped hiding the
name</Cite>: every page of the 90-page hearing transcript and the response-to-comments is
captioned *"Hatchworks LLC — Google Data Center — Fort Wayne: Project Zodiac Phase 3,"* and the
hearing itself was noticed as *"PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING PROJECT ZODIAC PHASE 3 (GOOGLE DATA
CENTER)."* `[verified]`

That upgrades **Hatchworks = Google** from an inference to a document-grounded fact. The same
record names the humans: the applicant Marc Stern of Hatchworks LLC, and the agent Heather
Dardinger of EMH&T. `[verified]`

<Callout variant="takeaway">
  The campus measures **855.8 acres** by GIS across the eleven parcels; the §401 calls it an
  "858-acre parcel" — within 0.3%, a clean cross-check. None of this came from a press release. It
  came from a deed chain and a permit caption: two records, two agencies, read together.
</Callout>
