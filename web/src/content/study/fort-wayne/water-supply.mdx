This is the network's sharpest version of the chapter's gap: the facility is not proposed
but <strong>operating</strong>. Project Zodiac holds an IDEM Title V air permit (003-47378-00530,
issued 2024-09-06) whose 34 emergency generators ground the IT-load bracket — the air side
of the record is instrument-grade. The water side is empty: no cooling method disclosed, no
water-balance scenario computable, no discharge characterization filed. A campus can reach
its operating permit with its entire water account off the record — that asymmetry is this
chapter's finding.

<Callout variant="open">
  The permit that grounds the load names the operator only as "a stationary data center."
  The records that would open this chapter are the same ordinary ones every gap panel
  names: a water-service contract, a wastewater permit, or the cooling-plant spec.
</Callout>
