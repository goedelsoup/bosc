Allen County held 49,690 jobs across 2,586 establishments in 2024. Ten years earlier it held
49,814. The county's employment has been flat for a decade.

Population moved in one direction over the same period. Allen County counted 106,586 people
in 2010 and 101,685 in 2023. So the county did not lose jobs. It lost people, and the jobs
that stayed did not grow.

That is the shape any jobs claim about this campus has to be read against. A promise of a few
hundred permanent positions divides into a base of about 50,000. It lands in a county whose
job count has not moved in ten years.

## What the county already does well

Manufacturing is the county's export sector, and it is not a small one. Allen County's
manufacturing employment is 2.15 times the national concentration, and it pays $92,093 a year
on average. The county-wide average across all sectors is $58,790, and the median household
income is $62,001.

Those figures matter because a jobs comparison is not only about counting. A job that pays
$92,000 and a job that pays less are not interchangeable in a local economy, whatever the
headcount says.
