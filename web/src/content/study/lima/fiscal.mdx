Lima is the network's proof that this chapter's blanks are not rhetorical. The record
shows the public side extended a 15-year tax abatement, public roadwork, and a sales-tax
exemption toward the project — and the county's own cost-benefit analysis behind that
trade is <strong>withheld</strong> (public-records request item 4, tracked as
<code>PRR-04</code> on the leads board). The four withheld or non-binding inputs move the
public's give by tens of millions of dollars; the economic-ledger annex lets you turn
each one and watch the band collapse as a record is produced.
