The cooling claim on this record is specific: at the February 2026 City of Urbana
meeting, the developer disclosed closed-loop cooling with water use "comparable to a
standard office building" — a <code>[reference]</code>-register statement, an operator's
own characterization, not an instrument. The platform's cooling-reconciliation harness
carries this site as its test case: at screening grade a sealed closed-loop, dry-rejection
campus predicts <strong>~0 MGD</strong> consumptive draw (initial fill plus minor leakage
makeup only), and the harness's outcome for Urbana is a <strong>gap</strong> — no
documented makeup or blowdown exists against which the claim can be tested, and no air
permit is on file that would show or rule out permitted cooling-tower drift.

<Callout variant="open">
  The records that would test the claim are ordinary ones: the industrial-pretreatment
  (indirect-discharge) permit, the sewer-use agreement and any blowdown authorization,
  the metered water-service account, and a cooling-tower blowdown DMR. Until one is
  produced, the claim keeps its register — a claim is not an instrument, and this study
  does not upgrade it.
</Callout>
