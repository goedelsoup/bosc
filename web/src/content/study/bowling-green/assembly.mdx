Twelve contiguous parcels in Middleton Township — **775.020 deeded acres**, whose union is a single
polygon about 1,821 by 2,489 metres — all carry the same owner of record:
<Cite record="bowling-green/land-assembly.yaml">**LIAMES, LLC**</Cite>. `[verified]`

**Eight of the twelve mail their tax bills to `1 META WAY, MENLO PARK, CA 94025`.** `[verified]`
That is Meta's own headquarters sitting in a county tax field, independent of any announcement.
Four of the twelve go somewhere else entirely, to 52 E Gay St, Columbus. A billing address is not
ownership of the LLC, and the distance between those two statements is the whole discipline of this
chapter.

## What the layer proves, and the three things it structurally cannot

This register is compiled from the Wood County auditor's CAMA parcel layer, not from a stack of
deed images — a *compiled register*, not an *instrument read*. Reading it honestly means reading
its limits first.

1. **It keeps only the last conveyance per parcel**, so every grantor is invisible. The two
   2025-04-09 quitclaim consolidations carrying 519 of the 775 acres folded in eleven predecessor
   tracts and erased their purchase prices. The $6,921,460 of surviving consideration is a floor on
   a fraction — never what the assembly cost.
2. **It has no `editingInfo`, so it must be dated from the data.** The maximum sale date across all
   73,839 rows is 2025-07-25, with zero rows after it. Every negative drawn from this layer is a
   statement about July 2025 and nothing later.
3. **It serves one row per polygon PART.** Deduping on parcel id drops land; summing the acreage
   double-counts. Union the geometry, take the acreage once.
