On **7 July 2026** the Middleton Township Trustees rezoned thirteen parcels to M-1 Light
Industrial. Every account you will find says the vote was **2–1 in favour**, with Moulton and
Vetter for and Petrea against.

Open the minutes and read the motion. It was **"to approve the recommendation of the Middleton
Township Zoning Commission"** — and that commission had voted, on 10 June, to **deny**. The roll
call: **Moulton NO, Petrea YES, Vetter NO.** `[verified]`

**1–2 against a denial.** The rezoning passed, and the minutes say so plainly — "It is noted the
application was approved for rezoning" — but the tally, the majority and the dissenter's name are
all the inverse of the reported ones. Petrea, named everywhere as the one who voted against, is the
only trustee who voted yes.

> **The skill:** a roll call is meaningless without the question. Never carry a tally forward
> without the motion text attached to it. Whether the framing was an error or a device, the minutes
> do not say — so neither do we.

## Then read what the same archive says about 2023

Search this township's published minutes for "Liames" and you find nothing before **September
2023**. Yet by 20 September the abatement application already covered "the **rezoned** properties
within the CRA boundaries," and by March 2024 the zoning inspector describes Liames as owning "the
recently rezoned parcels between St. Rt. 582, St. Rt. 25 and Mercer Road."

The rezonings that made the campus possible are docketed under the **landowners' own names** — Tim
Getz, ~80 acres, approved 3–0 on 2023-03-29; the Busch family, ~44 acres, approved 2–0 on
2023-05-03 with one trustee abstaining. `[verified]`

This is a **search hazard, not concealment.** A landowner rezoning his own land is ordinary. But it
is exactly why a name-keyed sweep of a public record will miss a project's first eighteen months,
and it is the most transferable lesson in this story.

## The sweep is what makes the negatives real

All **115** minutes files this township published for 2023–2026 were downloaded and grepped;
**fifteen** name Liames or Project Accordion. That is what licenses the negatives: there is no
~112-acre Devils Hole Road rezoning in this record, and there are no 279- or 402-acre 2023
resolutions — the two 2023 rezonings total 124 acres between them. `[open]`

> **The skill:** a stated sweep converts "we didn't find it" into "it isn't there." An unstated one
> converts it into nothing at all.
